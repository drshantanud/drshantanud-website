Mixed Sprouts Chaat

Mixed IndianTangy, Spicy, SavouryMedium spice

Lightly steamed sprouted moong, matki and chana with onion, tomato, cucumber and lemon.

Regional background: A common Indian sprouts salad.

Serves 3 · Total time 15 min · Easy · Method: No-cook / fresh, Steamed
Advance preparation: Sprout moong, matki and chana 1 to 2 days
Equipment: steamer, kadhai or non-stick pan, knife and chopping board

Add to a meal:

Method

  1. Steam the sprouts for 5 to 7 minutes until just tender.
  2. Cool and toss with onion, tomato and cucumber.
  3. Season with salt, chaat masala and lemon juice.
  4. Top with coriander.

Substitutions

  • Any sprouted pulse mix.

Tips

  • Always cook or steam sprouts lightly if you have a sensitive stomach.
  • To raise protein, serve with curd, a small portion of paneer or tofu, or a bowl of dal or sprouts.
