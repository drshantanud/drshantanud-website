Warm Sprout and Millet Kosambari Bowl

LunchVegetarianGood sourceL18

A modern bowl built on Karnataka's kosambari salad: sprouted moong, kala chana and foxtail millet, crunchy vegetables, a mustard-seed tempering and a curd dressing.

Ingredients

  • 60 g whole green moong, dry, sprouted · 50 g kala chana, dry · 60 g foxtail millet
  • 100 g cucumber · 60 g carrot · 60 g tomato, all diced · 15 g fresh grated coconut · 2 tbsp coriander · 1 tbsp lemon juice or 30 g grated raw mango
  • 15 g roasted peanuts, crushed
  • Tempering: 8 g groundnut oil · ½ tsp mustard seeds · 8 curry leaves · 1 green chilli · pinch of hing
  • Dressing: 150 g curd whisked with ½ tsp roasted cumin, salt and 1 tbsp chopped mint

Method

  1. Soak kala chana 8 hours; pressure-cook with 300 ml water for 5 whistles until tender. Drain.
  2. Rinse foxtail millet; cook with 180 ml water and a pinch of salt for 2 whistles (or 15 minutes covered on low). Fluff.
  3. Steam the moong sprouts 5 minutes until just tender.
  4. Heat oil; add mustard seeds, curry leaves, chilli and hing. Toss in the sprouts and chana for 2 minutes with salt.
  5. In each bowl, layer millet, then the warm pulses, then vegetables and coconut. Add lemon or raw mango.
  6. Top with peanuts and spoon over the curd dressing just before eating.

Protein optimisation

  • Moong sprouts supply 7 g per serving, kala chana 5 g, millet 3.5 g, curd 2.5 g, peanuts 2 g.
  • Boost: add 50 g grilled paneer or tofu cubes per bowl (+9 g or +7.5 g protein; +130 or +75 kcal).

Variations, serving and storage

  • Regional: Tamil sundal-bowl — swap the curd dressing for a sundal-style tempering with grated coconut only (dairy-free; 2.5 g less protein).
  • Substitution: quinoa, brown rice or little millet in place of foxtail millet.
  • Storage: components keep 3 days refrigerated, separately; assemble on the day.
  • Food safety: always cook sprouts for this bowl; pack curd dressing chilled and add just before eating.
  • Meal prep: excellent — the best lunch-box recipe in this chapter.
