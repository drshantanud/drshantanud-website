Chia and Almond Pudding

Mixed IndianSweetishMild spiceModern adaptation

Chia seeds soaked in milk with almonds, walnuts and dates, a lightly sweet snack or dessert.

Regional background: A modern adaptation; sweetened only with dates.

Serves 2 · Total time 10 min · Easy · Method: No-cook / fresh
Advance preparation: Set in the refrigerator for 4 hours or overnight
Equipment: kadhai or non-stick pan, knife and chopping board

Add to a meal:

Ingredients (for 2 servings)

IngredientQuantityHousehold measureProtein per serving
Chia seeds dry24 g2 tbsp2.0 g
Toned milk raw300 g1¼ cups4.7 g
Almonds raw20 g¼ cup2.1 g

Dates (seedless) dry20 g1 dates0.3 g

Quantities are in the raw or dry state shown, so water absorbed during cooking is not counted twice. Also used, not counted in nutrition (negligible): cardamom.

Method

  1. Mix chia seeds with milk and chopped dates.
  2. Stir well and refrigerate for 4 hours or overnight.
  3. Stir again and top with chopped almonds and walnuts.

Substitutions

  • Unsweetened soy milk.

Tips

  • Skip the dates for a no-sugar version.
  • To raise protein, serve with curd, a small portion of paneer or tofu, or a bowl of dal or sprouts.

Storage and reheating

Refrigerate up to 2 days.
