Soya Chunk Dum Pulao

LunchVegetarianHigh proteinL14

A Hyderabadi-inspired dum pulao in which curd-marinated soy chunks take the place of meat, layered with basmati rice, mint and fried onions.

Ingredients

  • 75 g soy chunks, dry · 150 g basmati rice
  • Marinade: 150 g curd · 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste · 1 tsp red chilli powder · ½ tsp turmeric · 5 g biryani masala · 3 g salt
  • 150 g onion, thinly sliced · 80 g green peas · 60 g carrot, diced · 15 g mint and 15 g coriander, chopped · 2 green chillies, slit
  • 15 g groundnut oil · whole spices: 1 bay leaf, 4 cloves, 4 green cardamoms, 1 cinnamon stick, 1 tsp shahi jeera
  • 3 g salt for the rice · 300 ml water · a few saffron strands in 1 tbsp warm milk (optional)
  • Raita: 240 g curd and 210 g grated cucumber for 3, with roasted cumin and salt

Method

  1. Boil soy chunks in 1 litre water for 5 minutes. Drain, rinse in cold water and squeeze hard to remove excess liquid; this removes the beany taste.
  2. Mix chunks with the marinade. Rest 30 minutes.
  3. Rinse basmati until the water runs clear; soak 20 minutes.
  4. Heat oil in a heavy pot. Fry half the onion on medium for 10 minutes until golden and crisp; remove for garnish.
  5. Add whole spices and the rest of the onion; cook 5 minutes. Add the marinated chunks, peas, carrot and chillies. Cook 6–8 minutes until the curd is absorbed and the masala coats the chunks.
  6. Add drained rice, mint, coriander, salt and 300 ml water. Bring to a boil.
  7. Cover tightly (seal with a foil layer under the lid). Cook on the lowest heat 15 minutes, then rest off the heat 10 minutes.
  8. Fluff gently; top with fried onion and saffron milk. Serve with raita.

Protein optimisation

  • Soy chunks supply 13 g per serving; rice 3.5 g; curd 2 g; peas 2 g.
  • Boost: increase soy chunks to 100 g dry (+4 g protein, +30 kcal per serving).

Variations, serving and storage

  • Regional: Kolkata-style — add 1 boiled potato per person and a pinch of nutmeg and mace.
  • Substitution: paneer cubes (150 g, added at step 6) for a soy-free version (about 4 g less protein and 4 g more fat per serving).
  • Storage: 2 days refrigerated.
  • Food safety: cooked rice should be cooled quickly and refrigerated within 1 hour; reheat until steaming hot. Do not reheat more than once.
  • Meal prep: good.
