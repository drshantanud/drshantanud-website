Ragi-Peanut-Date Ladoo

DessertVegetarianGood sourceDS12

Karnataka's nourishing ragi ladoo, made with roasted finger-millet flour, crushed peanuts and dates bound with a little ghee — a children's favourite and a sensible festival sweet.

Ingredients

  • 100 g ragi flour · 80 g roasted peanuts, coarsely crushed
  • 80 g soft dates, deseeded and finely chopped · 20 g skimmed milk powder
  • 15 g ghee · ½ tsp cardamom powder

Method

  1. Dry-roast ragi flour in a heavy pan on low for 8–10 minutes, stirring constantly, until it smells nutty and darkens slightly. Add 5 g ghee halfway through.
  2. Warm the remaining ghee; add the dates and mash over low heat for 2 minutes until soft and paste-like.
  3. Off the heat, mix the ragi, peanuts, milk powder and cardamom into the dates.
  4. While still warm, press firmly into 12 ladoos of about 20 g each. If they won't hold, add 1 tsp warm milk.
  5. Cool fully before storing.

Protein optimisation

  • Peanuts supply 3.5 g per serving, ragi 1 g, milk powder 1 g.
  • Boost: replace 30 g of the ragi with 30 g roasted chana powder (+1 g protein per serving), or serve 2 ladoos with a glass of milk (+8 g protein, +145 kcal).

Variations, serving and storage

  • Regional: traditional ragi unde with jaggery (40 g) and sesame instead of dates and peanuts.
  • Substitution: jowar or bajra flour for ragi.
  • Storage: 1 week in an airtight box; 2 weeks refrigerated.
  • Food safety: roast ragi thoroughly — raw ragi flour should not be eaten uncooked.
  • Meal prep: excellent.
