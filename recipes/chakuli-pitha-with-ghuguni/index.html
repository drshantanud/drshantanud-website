Chakuli Pitha with Ghuguni

BreakfastVegetarianGood sourceB19

Odisha's thin, lacy urad-rice crepe served with ghuguni, a spiced curry of dried yellow peas — a pairing enjoyed at home and at festival breakfasts.

Ingredients

  • Chakuli: 100 g raw rice · 50 g whole white urad dal · 3 g salt · 9 g groundnut oil (about 2 tsp) for cooking
  • Ghuguni: 120 g dried yellow or white peas · 80 g onion, chopped · 60 g tomato · 1 tsp ginger-garlic paste · 1 tsp cumin seeds · 1 bay leaf · ½ tsp turmeric · 1 tsp cumin-coriander powder · ½ tsp garam masala · 3 g salt · 10 g mustard oil (2 tsp)
  • Garnish: chopped onion, coriander, lemon

Method

  1. Soak rice and urad dal together, and the dried peas separately, for 6 hours.
  2. Grind rice and urad with 200 ml water to a smooth, thin batter. Add salt and ferment 8–10 hours until bubbly.
  3. Pressure-cook the peas with 400 ml water for 4–5 whistles until soft but holding shape.
  4. Heat mustard oil until it just smokes, then lower the heat. Add cumin and bay leaf, then onion; cook 5 minutes until golden.
  5. Add ginger-garlic paste, tomato and powdered spices. Cook 3–4 minutes until the oil separates.
  6. Add peas with their liquid; simmer 8–10 minutes, mashing a few peas to thicken. Finish with garam masala.
  7. Heat a tawa on medium-high and grease lightly. Pour 1 ladle of batter from the edge inward to make a thin, lacy crepe. Drizzle ¼ tsp oil, cover and cook 2 minutes. Do not flip.
  8. Serve 2 chakuli with ghuguni, garnished.

Protein optimisation

  • Dried peas supply 8.5 g per serving, urad 4 g, rice 2.5 g.
  • Boost: increase peas to 160 g dry (+3 g protein, +45 kcal per serving), or serve with 100 g curd (+3.5 g protein, +60 kcal).

Variations, serving and storage

  • Regional: Kolkata ghugni — top with chopped green chilli, tamarind water and a pinch of roasted cumin.
  • Substitution: kabuli chana in place of dried peas.
  • Storage: batter keeps 3 days refrigerated; ghuguni keeps 3 days refrigerated and freezes well.
  • Food safety: reheat ghuguni until bubbling.
  • Meal prep: excellent.
