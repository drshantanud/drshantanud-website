Muttai Kuzhambu (Egg Tamarind Curry)

DinnerContains eggGood sourceD18

A Tamil home curry: boiled eggs simmered in a tangy tamarind gravy with shallots, garlic and sesame oil, eaten with rice.

Ingredients

  • 5 large eggs, hard-boiled and peeled
  • 15 g tamarind, soaked in 300 ml warm water and squeezed · 80 g shallots, peeled · 80 g tomato · 6 garlic cloves
  • 12 g kuzhambu or sambar powder · ¼ tsp turmeric · 3 g jaggery · 4 g salt
  • 12 g gingelly (sesame) oil · ½ tsp mustard seeds · ¼ tsp methi seeds · 10 curry leaves
  • Plate: 100 g raw rice for 2

Method

  1. Make 3 shallow slits on each egg.
  2. Heat oil; add mustard and methi seeds, then curry leaves. Add shallots and garlic; cook 5 minutes until golden.
  3. Add tomato; cook 3 minutes until soft. Add kuzhambu powder and turmeric; stir 30 seconds.
  4. Add tamarind water, jaggery and salt. Simmer 10–12 minutes until the raw tamarind smell is gone and the gravy thickens and glistens with oil.
  5. Add eggs; simmer 5 minutes so they absorb the gravy. Serve with rice.

Protein optimisation

  • Eggs supply 16 g per serving; shallots, garlic and spices 2 g; rice 3.5 g on the plate.
  • Boost: add 1 more egg (+3 g protein, +36 kcal per serving), or serve with 100 g curd (+3.5 g protein, +60 kcal).

Variations, serving and storage

  • Regional: Chettinad style — add 1 tsp roasted fennel and pepper powder.
  • Substitution: groundnut oil instead of gingelly oil.
  • Storage: 2 days refrigerated; flavour deepens overnight.
  • Food safety: refrigerate within 2 hours.
  • Meal prep: good — make the gravy ahead; add eggs on the day.
