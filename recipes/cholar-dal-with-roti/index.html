Cholar Dal with Roti

LunchVegetarianGood sourceL10

Bengal's festive chana dal, cooked with coconut pieces, whole garam masala and a gentle sweetness; usually served with luchi, here with whole-wheat roti.

Ingredients

  • 150 g chana dal · ½ tsp turmeric · 5 g salt
  • 15 g fresh coconut, in small slivers · 10 g raisins · 5 g sugar
  • 10 g ghee · 1 tsp cumin seeds · 2 bay leaves · 2 dry red chillies · 3 green cardamoms · 3 cloves · 1 small cinnamon stick · 10 g ginger, grated · pinch of hing
  • Plate: 210 g whole-wheat atta for 6 rotis · 300 g curd

Method

  1. Soak chana dal 1 hour. Pressure-cook with turmeric and 500 ml water for 3 whistles; the grains should be soft but hold shape.
  2. Heat ghee; fry the coconut slivers until golden. Remove.
  3. In the same ghee, add cumin, bay leaves, red chillies and the whole spices for 30 seconds, then ginger and hing.
  4. Add the dal, 150 ml water, salt, sugar, raisins and coconut. Simmer 8 minutes until thick enough to coat a spoon.
  5. Serve with rotis and curd.

Protein optimisation

  • Chana dal supplies 10.5 g per serving, rotis 8 g, curd 3.5 g.
  • Boost: serve with a side of 100 g chhena or paneer bhurji (+18 g protein, +260 kcal for the side).
  • Lower sugar: omit the sugar and raisins; the dal is still traditional in many homes without them.

Variations, serving and storage

  • Regional: Odia version — omit sugar, add a little panch phoron.
  • Substitution: luchi for festive meals (about 120–150 kcal each, deep-fried).
  • Storage: 3 days refrigerated.
  • Food safety: reheat until boiling.
  • Meal prep: excellent.
