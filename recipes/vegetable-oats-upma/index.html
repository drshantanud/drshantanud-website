Vegetable Oats Upma

BreakfastVegetarianGood sourceE01

A quick savoury oats breakfast cooked like rava upma, with plenty of vegetables and a few peanuts for crunch. Ready in 20 minutes.

Ingredients

  • 80 g rolled oats
  • 60 g onion, chopped · 50 g carrot, diced · 40 g green peas · 40 g capsicum, diced · 40 g tomato, chopped
  • 10 g raw peanuts · 5 g urad dal · 1 tsp mustard seeds · 8 curry leaves · 1 green chilli · 5 g ginger, chopped
  • 8 g oil · ¼ tsp turmeric · 3 g salt · 180 ml hot water · 2 tsp lemon juice · coriander leaves

Method

  1. Dry-roast the oats in a kadhai on low heat for 3–4 minutes until lightly golden. Tip out onto a plate.
  2. Heat the oil. Add mustard seeds; when they splutter add urad dal and peanuts and fry until the dal turns golden. Add curry leaves, green chilli and ginger.
  3. Add the onion and cook 2–3 minutes. Add carrot, peas and capsicum, cover and cook 3 minutes.
  4. Add tomato, turmeric and salt and cook 2 minutes until soft.
  5. Pour in 180 ml hot water and bring to a boil. Add the roasted oats, stir and cook on low heat 2–3 minutes until the water is absorbed.
  6. Cover, rest 2 minutes, then finish with lemon juice and coriander.

Protein optimisation

  • Oats supply about 5 g protein per serving and peanuts and dal another 2 g.
  • Boost: serve with 100 g curd (+3 g protein, +73 kcal) or stir in 50 g crumbled paneer at the end (+5 g per serving).

Variations, serving and storage

  • Use 80 g broken wheat (dalia) instead of oats; cook 8–10 minutes with 250 ml water.
  • Storage: best eaten fresh; it thickens as it cools.
  • Diabetes: keep to one bowl and pair with curd or sprouts.
