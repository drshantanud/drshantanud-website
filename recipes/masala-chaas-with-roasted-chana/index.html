Masala Chaas with Roasted Chana

DrinkVegetarianGood sourceDR8

Gujarat's spiced buttermilk, made thicker than usual and blended with roasted chana powder, which adds body and a nutty depth along with plant protein.

Ingredients

  • 250 g toned-milk curd · 150 ml cold water
  • 15 g roasted chana (dalia), ground to a fine powder
  • ½ tsp roasted cumin powder · ¼ tsp black salt · pinch of salt · ½ tsp grated ginger · ½ green chilli · 1 tbsp chopped coriander and mint
  • Optional tempering: 2 g oil, ¼ tsp mustard seeds, 4 curry leaves, pinch of hing

Method

  1. Blend curd, water, chana powder, ginger, chilli and herbs for 20 seconds until frothy and smooth.
  2. Stir in cumin and both salts.
  3. For the tempering, heat oil, add mustard, curry leaves and hing, and pour over. Serve chilled.

Protein optimisation

  • Curd supplies 8 g; roasted chana 3 g.
  • Boost: 50 g hung curd in place of 50 g curd (+1 g protein, +15 kcal), or increase chana powder to 25 g (+2 g protein, +35 kcal; thicker).

Variations, serving and storage

  • Regional: Kerala sambharam — thinner, with crushed shallot, ginger, green chilli and curry leaves, no chana.
  • Substitution: sattu (15 g) in place of roasted chana powder.
  • Storage: 1 day refrigerated; whisk again before serving.
  • Food safety: keep chilled; discard if it smells unusually sour.
  • Meal prep: grind roasted chana powder in bulk; keeps 1 month airtight.
