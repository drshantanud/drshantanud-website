Mahabaleshwar Strawberry Yogurt Smoothie

DrinkVegetarianGood sourceDR4

Winter strawberries from Mahabaleshwar blended with hung curd and milk: tart, bright pink, and the lowest-calorie fruit smoothie in the chapter.

Good source without whey (just under 12 g); High protein with whey, or with 15 g skimmed milk powder (about 17 g protein, 260 kcal). Optional 5 g honey adds 15 kcal and 4 g sugar. Values checked against IFCT 2017 on 14 Oct 2026.

Ingredients

  • 150 g fresh strawberries, hulled (about 10 medium)
  • 120 g hung curd · 100 ml chilled toned milk
  • 3–4 ice cubes · optional: 5 g honey, a few mint leaves, 15 g unflavoured whey

Method

  1. Wash strawberries in running water and hull them.
  2. Blend strawberries, hung curd, milk and whey (if using) until smooth.
  3. Taste; add honey only if the berries are very tart. Add ice and blend briefly.
  4. Serve immediately, garnished with mint.

Protein optimisation

  • Hung curd supplies 7 g, milk 3 g, strawberries 1.5 g.
  • Boost: whey as above, 15 g skimmed milk powder (+5 g protein, +55 kcal, +8 g lactose), or 1 tbsp chia seeds soaked 10 minutes (+1.5 g protein, +50 kcal, +3.5 g fibre).

Variations, serving and storage

  • Regional: Mahabaleshwar "strawberry with cream" style — use 50 g malai in place of the milk (adds about 120 kcal, mostly fat).
  • Substitution: frozen strawberries; or jamun or mulberries in season.
  • Storage: drink within 2 hours.
  • Food safety: strawberries can carry soil; wash well under running water.
  • Meal prep: freeze hulled berries in season.
