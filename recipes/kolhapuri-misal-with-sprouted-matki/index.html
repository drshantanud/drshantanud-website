Kolhapuri Misal with Sprouted Matki

LunchVegetarianGood sourceL03

Kolhapur's fiery misal: sprouted moth beans in a red, coconut-spiced rassa (thin gravy), topped here with roasted chana instead of fried farsan.

Ingredients

  • 150 g matki (moth beans), dry, sprouted (see B11 for method; tails 3–5 mm)
  • Kat (rassa): 100 g onion, sliced · 15 g dry coconut (kopra), grated · 100 g tomato · 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste · 15 g Kolhapuri kanda-lasun masala (or 2 tsp red chilli powder plus 1 tsp goda masala) · ¼ tsp turmeric · 20 g groundnut oil · 5 g salt · 600 ml water
  • Topping: 30 g roasted chana (dalia) · 60 g onion, finely chopped · coriander · lemon wedges
  • Plate: 6 whole-wheat pav (about 240 g)

Method

  1. Pressure-cook the sprouts with 300 ml water and a pinch of salt for 1 whistle. They should be tender but whole.
  2. Dry-roast the coconut until brown; roast sliced onion in 5 g oil until dark brown. Grind both with tomato into a smooth paste.
  3. Heat the remaining oil. Add ginger-garlic paste for 1 minute, then the ground paste, turmeric and Kolhapuri masala. Cook on medium 8–10 minutes, until oil rises to the surface.
  4. Add 600 ml water and salt; simmer 10 minutes. This thin, red gravy is the kat.
  5. Mix a third of the kat into the sprouts and simmer 5 minutes.
  6. To serve: sprouts in a bowl, a ladle of kat poured over, then raw onion, roasted chana, coriander and lemon. Serve with pav.

Protein optimisation

  • Matki supplies 11 g per serving; roasted chana 2 g; pav 8 g on the full plate.
  • Boost: top with 30 g crumbled paneer per serving (+5.5 g protein, +80 kcal), or serve a glass of chaas made from 150 g curd (+5 g protein, +90 kcal).

Variations, serving and storage

  • Regional: Puneri misal — milder kat with a little jaggery and potato in the usal.
  • Substitution: sprouted moong or a mixed sprout blend.
  • Lighter plate: 1 pav instead of 2 saves about 110 kcal.
  • Storage: usal and kat keep 2 days refrigerated, separately.
  • Food safety: cook sprouts thoroughly; discard any that smell sour.
  • Meal prep: good — make kat ahead; sprout matki on the weekend.
