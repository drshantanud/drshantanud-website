Rava Uppittu with Peas, Peanuts and Paneer

BreakfastVegetarianGood sourceB14

Karnataka's uppittu, with green peas and cubes of paneer folded in, becomes a filling breakfast with three times the protein of a plain upma.

Ingredients

  • 80 g Bombay rava (sooji) · 80 g full-fat paneer, in 1 cm cubes · 60 g green peas · 15 g peanuts
  • 50 g onion, 40 g carrot, 40 g beans, all finely chopped
  • 10 g groundnut oil (2 tsp) — ghee is traditional · ½ tsp mustard seeds · 5 g urad dal · 5 g chana dal · 10 curry leaves · 2 green chillies · 1 tsp grated ginger
  • 400 ml water · 3 g salt · 1 tbsp lemon juice · 2 tbsp coriander

Method

  1. Dry-roast rava in a kadhai on medium-low for 5–6 minutes, stirring, until fragrant and very lightly coloured. Set aside.
  2. Heat oil. Add mustard seeds; when they pop, add urad dal, chana dal and peanuts. Fry 1–2 minutes until golden.
  3. Add curry leaves, chillies, ginger and onion. Cook 3 minutes until soft.
  4. Add carrot, beans and peas; cook 2 minutes.
  5. Add water and salt; bring to a rolling boil.
  6. Lower the heat. Pour in the rava in a thin stream, stirring constantly to prevent lumps.
  7. Fold in the paneer. Cover and cook on low for 2–3 minutes until the rava is fluffy and the water absorbed.
  8. Finish with lemon juice and coriander.

Protein optimisation

  • Paneer supplies 7 g per serving, rava 4.5 g, peas 2 g, peanuts and dals 3 g.
  • Boost: increase paneer to 120 g (+3.5 g protein, +50 kcal, +4 g fat per serving), or serve with 100 g curd (+3.5 g protein, +60 kcal).

Variations, serving and storage

  • Regional: Tamil Nadu rava kichadi — add tomato and ½ tsp sambar powder and finish with 5 g ghee.
  • Substitution: broken wheat (dalia) or vermicelli in place of rava; adjust water to package directions.
  • Storage: 1 day refrigerated; reheat with a sprinkle of water, covered.
  • Food safety: do not leave cooked uppittu with paneer at room temperature for more than 2 hours.
  • Meal prep: roast rava in bulk; it keeps 1 month in an airtight jar.
