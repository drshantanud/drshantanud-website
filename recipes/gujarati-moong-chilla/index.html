Gujarati Moong Dal Chilla

GujaratiSavoury, SpicyMedium spice

Savoury pancakes from soaked moong dal with ginger and chilli.

Regional background: A popular Gujarati breakfast.

Serves 3 · Total time 25 min · Easy · Method: Sautéed
Advance preparation: Soak moong dal 2 hours
Equipment: kadhai or non-stick pan, knife and chopping board

Add to a meal:

Quantities are in the raw or dry state shown, so water absorbed during cooking is not counted twice. Also used, not counted in nutrition (negligible): salt, green chilli, turmeric, water.

Method

  1. Soak moong dal for 2 hours and grind with ginger and chilli.
  2. Add salt, turmeric, onion and coriander.
  3. Spread on a hot non-stick tawa.
  4. Drizzle a little oil and cook both sides until golden.

Substitutions

  • Add grated paneer as a filling to raise protein.

Tips

  • Serve with green chutney or curd.

Storage and reheating

Batter keeps 1 day refrigerated.
