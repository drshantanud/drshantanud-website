Cocoa-Peanut Shake

DrinkVegetarianGood sourceDR6

A chocolate-peanut milkshake sweetened only with two dates, thick enough to feel like a treat. Whey is optional.

Good source without whey; High protein with whey. The 2 dates add about 14 g sugar — dates are whole fruit with some fibre, but their sugar counts like any other sweetener. Values checked against IFCT 2017 on 14 Oct 2026.

Ingredients

  • 250 ml chilled toned milk · 10 g unsweetened cocoa powder (1½ tbsp) · 16 g unsweetened peanut butter (1 tbsp)
  • 2 soft dates (about 20 g, deseeded), soaked 10 minutes in 2 tbsp hot milk
  • Pinch of salt · 3–4 ice cubes · optional: 15 g unflavoured whey

Method

  1. Soak the dates in 2 tbsp hot milk from the measured milk until soft.
  2. Blend dates with their milk and the cocoa to a smooth paste — this prevents cocoa lumps.
  3. Add the rest of the milk, peanut butter, salt, whey (if using) and ice. Blend 45 seconds until frothy.

Protein optimisation

  • Milk supplies 8 g, peanut butter 4 g, cocoa 2 g.
  • Boost without whey: 50 g hung curd (+3 g protein, +45 kcal) gives a thicker, mousse-like shake.

Variations, serving and storage

  • Regional flavour: "Mysore-pak" style — a pinch of cardamom and 1 tsp ghee-roasted besan blended in (+25 kcal).
  • Substitution: roasted peanuts (20 g) for peanut butter; 5 g jaggery for the dates (similar sugar, less fibre).
  • Storage: drink fresh; refrigerate up to 12 hours.
  • Food safety: use pasteurised milk.
  • Meal prep: pre-mix cocoa, dates and peanut butter into a paste; keeps 3 days refrigerated.
