High protein: at least 12 g protein per serving (20 g for a lunch or dinner plate) and at least 5 g per 100 kcal. Good source: at least 6 g per serving (10 g for a plate) that does not meet the High-protein test. These are this collection's own labels, not regulatory claims.

Nutrition values are estimates calculated from the Indian Food Composition Tables 2017 (ICMR-NIN) and typical product labels. They have not been laboratory-analysed and will vary with your ingredients, brands and cooking. Recipes are for healthy adults and are general information only, not a substitute for advice from a doctor or qualified dietitian. If you are pregnant or breastfeeding, cooking for children, have food allergies, or live with kidney disease, diabetes or any other medical condition, please take professional advice about your diet.
