Dhaba Dal Tadka (Mixed Dals)

DinnerVegetarianGood sourceD02

The highway dhaba favourite: toor, masoor and chana dals cooked soft and finished with a smoky double tadka of ghee, garlic and dried red chillies.

Ingredients

  • 75 g toor dal · 50 g masoor dal · 35 g chana dal
  • 80 g onion · 120 g tomato, chopped · 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste · 2 green chillies · ½ tsp turmeric · 5 g salt
  • Tadka: 10 g ghee · 1 tsp cumin seeds · 4 garlic cloves, sliced · 2 dry red chillies · pinch of hing · ½ tsp Kashmiri chilli powder · ½ tsp garam masala · 1 tsp kasuri methi
  • Plate: 105 g whole-wheat atta for 3 rotis · raita of 450 g curd and 150 g cucumber

Method

  1. Soak the dals together 30 minutes. Pressure-cook with turmeric, salt and 700 ml water for 4 whistles. Whisk lightly; it should be creamy but not smooth.
  2. Heat half the ghee; cook onion 5 minutes until golden. Add ginger-garlic paste, chillies and tomato; cook 5 minutes until soft. Add to the dal and simmer 8 minutes.
  3. For the final tadka, heat the remaining ghee until hot. Add cumin, garlic, red chillies and hing; when the garlic turns golden, take the pan off the heat and add chilli powder, garam masala and kasuri methi.
  4. Pour the tadka over the dal at the table. Serve with roti and raita.

Protein optimisation

  • The three dals supply 12 g per serving; roti 4 g and raita 5.5 g on the plate.
  • Boost: use hung curd for the raita (+7 g protein, +60 kcal per serving), which lifts the plate to High protein.

Variations, serving and storage

  • Regional: Dal fry — omit the second tadka and cook the dal thicker, with more onion and tomato.
  • Smoky dhungar method: place a hot piece of charcoal in a small steel bowl on the dal, pour ½ tsp ghee on it, and cover for 2 minutes.
  • Storage: 3 days refrigerated; freezes 2 months.
  • Food safety: reheat until boiling.
  • Meal prep: excellent.
