Soyabean Bori Dalna

DinnerVegetarianHigh proteinD16

A Bengali home curry of soy chunks and potato in a mustard-oil, cumin-ginger gravy finished with garam masala — a weeknight staple in Kolkata kitchens.

Ingredients

  • 75 g soy chunks, dry · 150 g potato, in 3 cm cubes
  • 80 g onion, puréed · 100 g tomato, puréed · 1 tbsp ginger paste · 1 tsp cumin paste (or cumin powder) · 50 g curd
  • 12 g mustard oil · 1 bay leaf · 1 tsp cumin seeds · 2 dry red chillies · ½ tsp turmeric · 1 tsp Kashmiri chilli powder · ½ tsp garam masala · 2 g sugar · 5 g salt
  • Plate: 210 g whole-wheat atta for 6 rotis

Method

  1. Boil soy chunks in 1 litre water for 5 minutes. Drain, rinse in cold water and squeeze dry.
  2. Rub chunks with a pinch of turmeric and salt. Heat mustard oil until it just smokes, lower the heat, and fry chunks 3 minutes until lightly browned. Remove; fry potatoes 5 minutes.
  3. Add bay leaf, cumin seeds and red chillies, then onion purée; cook 5 minutes.
  4. Add ginger and cumin pastes, tomato purée, turmeric and chilli powder; cook 4 minutes until oil separates.
  5. Lower the heat and stir in whisked curd. Add chunks, potato, sugar, salt and 300 ml water.
  6. Cover and simmer 10 minutes until the potato is tender. Finish with garam masala. Serve with rotis.

Protein optimisation

  • Soy chunks supply 13 g per serving; potato, curd and vegetables 2 g; rotis 8 g on the plate.
  • Boost: add 80 g green peas with the potatoes (+2 g protein, +25 kcal per serving).

Variations, serving and storage

  • Regional: soyabean kosha — cooked down until dry and dark, with more onion.
  • Substitution: 150 g paneer for the soy chunks (soy-free; about 4 g less protein and 10 g more fat per serving).
  • Storage: 3 days refrigerated.
  • Food safety: reheat until steaming throughout.
  • Meal prep: excellent.
