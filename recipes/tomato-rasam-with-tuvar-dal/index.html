Tomato Rasam with Tuvar Dal

DalVegetarianE08

A thin, peppery tomato-tamarind rasam with cooked tuvar dal stirred in for body and protein. Served with rice or as a soup.

Ingredients

  • 60 g tuvar (arhar) dal · 200 g ripe tomato, chopped · 15 g tamarind pulp
  • 8 g rasam powder (or 1 tsp each coriander seeds, cumin and black pepper, roasted and crushed) · 5 g garlic, crushed
  • Tempering: 8 g oil or ghee · 1 tsp mustard seeds · ½ tsp cumin · pinch of hing · 8 curry leaves · 1 dry red chilli
  • ¼ tsp turmeric · 4 g salt · 10 g coriander leaves · about 700 ml water

Method

  1. Pressure-cook the dal with 200 ml water and turmeric until very soft; mash well.
  2. Soak tamarind in 100 ml warm water and squeeze out the juice.
  3. Boil tomato, tamarind water, garlic, rasam powder and salt with 300 ml water for 8–10 minutes.
  4. Add the mashed dal and 100 ml water; heat until it just froths. Do not boil hard.
  5. Heat oil, add mustard, cumin, hing, curry leaves and red chilli, and pour over the rasam. Add coriander and cover for 2 minutes.

Protein optimisation

  • Each bowl gives about 4 g protein; the dal is the main source.
  • Boost: serve with 1 katori kootu or a dal side, or increase tuvar dal to 90 g (+1.5 g per bowl).

Variations, serving and storage

  • Pepper rasam: double the pepper and leave out the tomato.
  • Storage: 2 days refrigerated.
  • Blood pressure: rasam can be salty; keep salt to the amount given.
