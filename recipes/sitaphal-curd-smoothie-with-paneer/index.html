Sitaphal-Curd Smoothie with Paneer

DrinkVegetarianGood sourceDR10

Custard apple from the Deccan and central Indian plateau, blended with hung curd, milk and a little fresh paneer — creamy, naturally sweet, and higher in protein than it tastes.

Ingredients

  • 100 g sitaphal (custard apple) pulp, deseeded (from 1 large fruit)
  • 100 g hung curd · 100 ml chilled toned milk · 20 g fresh paneer · optional: 15 g unflavoured whey
  • Pinch of cardamom · 2–3 ice cubes

Method

  1. Scoop the pulp from the fruit. Remove every seed by pressing the segments through your fingers or a coarse sieve — the seeds must never be blended, as they are toxic when crushed.
  2. Blend the pulp with hung curd, milk, paneer, cardamom and whey (if using) until silky, about 45 seconds.
  3. Add ice and blend briefly. Serve at once; custard apple darkens on standing.

Protein optimisation

  • Hung curd supplies 6 g, paneer 4 g, milk 3 g, fruit 1.5 g.
  • Boost: add 15 g whey (+12 g protein, +60 kcal), or 15 g skimmed milk powder (+5 g protein, +55 kcal, +8 g lactose), or increase paneer to 40 g (+4 g protein, +50 kcal, +4 g fat).

Variations, serving and storage

  • Regional: sitaphal basundi-style — blend with 150 ml reduced milk and serve as a dessert drink (higher in calories).
  • Substitution: ripe guava (strained) or papaya when sitaphal is out of season.
  • Storage: drink within 1 hour; freeze deseeded pulp for up to 2 months.
  • Food safety: discard all seeds; never grind them.
  • Meal prep: deseed and freeze pulp in 100 g portions during the season.
