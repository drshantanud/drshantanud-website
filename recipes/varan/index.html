Maharashtrian Varan

MaharashtrianSavoury, Non-spicyMild spice

Plain, comforting toor dal tempered with a little ghee and asafoetida.

Regional background: Everyday Maharashtrian dal, traditionally served with rice and ghee.

Serves 4 · Total time 25 min · Easy · Method: Pressure-cooked
Equipment: pressure cooker, kadhai or non-stick pan, knife and chopping board

Quantities are in the raw or dry state shown, so water absorbed during cooking is not counted twice. Also used, not counted in nutrition (negligible): salt, turmeric, asafoetida, water.

Method

  1. Wash toor dal and pressure cook with turmeric and 3 cups water for 3 whistles.
  2. Mash well.
  3. Heat ghee, add asafoetida and a pinch of spices.
  4. Pour over the dal, add salt and coriander and simmer 5 minutes.

Substitutions

  • Moong dal for a lighter dal.

Tips

  • Serve with a salad and a vegetable to add fibre.
  • To raise protein, serve with curd, a small portion of paneer or tofu, or a bowl of dal or sprouts.

Storage and reheating

Refrigerate up to 2 days.
