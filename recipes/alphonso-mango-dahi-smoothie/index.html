Alphonso Mango-Dahi Smoothie

DrinkVegetarianGood sourceDR1

Ratnagiri Alphonso blended with thick hung curd, a little milk and cardamom — a summer lassi-smoothie that gets its sweetness entirely from the mango.

Good source without whey; High protein with optional whey (about 23 g) or with 15 g skimmed milk powder (about 16 g; see Boost). All sugars are natural (about 11 g from mango, 10 g lactose). Kesar mango is the IFCT proxy for Alphonso. Values checked against IFCT 2017 on 14 Oct 2026.

Ingredients

  • 120 g ripe Alphonso mango flesh (about 1 small mango)
  • 120 g hung curd (from about 240 g toned-milk curd)
  • 100 ml chilled toned milk
  • ¼ tsp cardamom powder · 3–4 ice cubes · a few saffron strands (optional) · optional: 15 g unflavoured whey

Method

  1. Chop the mango flesh; freeze for 30 minutes if you want a thicker, colder drink.
  2. Blend mango, hung curd, milk, cardamom and whey (if using) until completely smooth, about 45 seconds.
  3. Add ice and blend 10 seconds more. Taste: a ripe Alphonso needs no sugar.
  4. Pour and top with saffron.

Protein optimisation

  • Hung curd supplies 7 g; milk 3 g; mango 0.5 g.
  • Boost: add 15 g whey (+12 g protein, +60 kcal), or 15 g skimmed milk powder (+5 g protein, +55 kcal, +8 g lactose).

Variations, serving and storage

  • Regional: Punjabi mango lassi — use 200 g plain curd and no milk; thinner and tangier (about 6 g less protein).
  • Substitution: frozen Alphonso pulp (check for added sugar) out of season; Greek yogurt for hung curd.
  • Storage: drink within 2 hours; refrigerate up to 12 hours (it thickens).
  • Food safety: refrigerate hung curd while straining.
  • Meal prep: freeze mango cubes in season for year-round use.
