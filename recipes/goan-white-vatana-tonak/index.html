Goan White Vatana Tonak

DinnerVegetarianGood sourceD09

A Goan Hindu home favourite: dried white peas simmered in a dark, roasted-coconut tonak masala with a hint of tamarind, eaten with Goan pav.

Ingredients

  • 180 g dried white peas (safed vatana)
  • 100 g onion, sliced · 80 g tomato · 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste · 5 g tamarind · ¼ tsp turmeric · 5 g salt
  • Tonak masala: 30 g grated coconut · 1 tbsp coriander seeds · 1 tsp cumin · 1 tsp poppy seeds · 4 Kashmiri chillies · 4 cloves · 1 small cinnamon stick · ½ tsp black pepper
  • 10 g groundnut oil
  • Plate: 3 whole-wheat pav · 300 g curd

Method

  1. Soak the peas 8 hours. Pressure-cook with turmeric and 600 ml water for 3–4 whistles until soft but whole.
  2. Dry-roast the coconut until deep brown; dry-roast the whole spices for 2 minutes. Grind with 100 ml water to a smooth paste.
  3. Heat oil; cook onion 8 minutes until browned. Add ginger-garlic paste and tomato; cook 4 minutes.
  4. Add the tonak paste; cook on low 5 minutes until fragrant and the oil separates.
  5. Add peas with their liquid, tamarind and salt. Simmer 10 minutes until thick.
  6. Serve with pav and curd.

Protein optimisation

  • Dried peas supply 12.5 g per serving; pav 4 g and curd 3.5 g on the plate.
  • Boost: add 100 g paneer cubes in the last 5 minutes (+6 g protein, +85 kcal per serving).

Variations, serving and storage

  • Regional: Goan ross omelette-style plate — serve with a thin egg omelette for an egg-inclusive version (+6 g protein per egg).
  • Substitution: black-eyed beans or kabuli chana.
  • Storage: 3 days refrigerated; freezes 2 months; flavour improves on day 2.
  • Food safety: reheat until boiling.
  • Meal prep: excellent.
