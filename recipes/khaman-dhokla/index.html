Khaman Dhokla

SnackVegetarianGood sourceS03

Gujarat's spongy steamed besan cake, tempered with mustard seeds and chillies; a soft, light snack that delivers useful protein from besan alone.

Ingredients

  • 150 g besan (fine) · 60 g curd · 150 ml water · 1 tsp ginger-green chilli paste · ¼ tsp turmeric · 10 g sugar · 1 tbsp lemon juice · 4 g salt · 1 tsp (5–6 g) fruit salt (Eno) or ¾ tsp baking soda
  • Tempering: 10 g groundnut oil · 1 tsp mustard seeds · 5 g sesame seeds · 10 curry leaves · 2 green chillies, slit · 80 ml water · 5 g sugar
  • Garnish: 10 g fresh grated coconut · 2 tbsp coriander

Method

  1. Set up a steamer with 5 cm of water and bring to a boil. Grease a 20 cm round tin.
  2. Whisk besan, curd, water, ginger-chilli paste, turmeric, sugar, lemon and salt into a smooth, thick batter (like idli batter). Rest 10 minutes.
  3. Just before steaming, add fruit salt and 1 tsp water on top of it; whisk briskly in one direction for 10 seconds. The batter will foam and nearly double.
  4. Pour immediately into the tin. Steam on high 15–18 minutes, until a skewer comes out clean. Rest 5 minutes and cut into 16 squares.
  5. Heat oil; add mustard seeds, sesame, curry leaves and chillies. Add 80 ml water and 5 g sugar; boil 30 seconds.
  6. Spoon the tempering evenly over the dhokla so it soaks in. Garnish and serve.

Protein optimisation

  • Besan supplies 8 g per serving; curd 0.5 g.
  • Boost: serve with 75 g hung-curd mint dip (+4 g protein, +70 kcal), or replace 30 g of the besan with 30 g moong dal flour (similar protein, softer crumb).

Variations, serving and storage

  • Regional: Surti-style — sprinkle crushed black pepper and a little red chilli powder before steaming.
  • Substitution: sour buttermilk (100 ml) in place of curd and water for tang.
  • Storage: 2 days refrigerated; steam 2 minutes to refresh.
  • Food safety: refrigerate within 2 hours in hot weather.
  • Meal prep: good.
