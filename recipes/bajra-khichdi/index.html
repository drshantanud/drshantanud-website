Bajra Khichdi

RajasthaniSavoury, Non-spicyMild spice

Cracked pearl millet cooked with moong dal until soft.

Regional background: A traditional Rajasthani winter dish. Uses bajra flour as a simple stand-in for cracked bajra.

Serves 3 · Total time 40 min · Easy · Method: Pressure-cooked
Advance preparation: Soak bajra 4 hours (or use bajra dalia)
Equipment: pressure cooker, kadhai or non-stick pan, knife and chopping board

Method

  1. Dry roast bajra for 3 minutes.
  2. Wash moong dal and pressure cook with bajra, turmeric and salt, 4 cups water, for 4 whistles.
  3. Heat ghee, add cumin and spices, and pour over.
  4. Mix and serve with curd.

Substitutions

  • Cracked bajra (bajra dalia) gives better texture.

Tips

  • Serve with curd or kadhi.
  • To raise protein, serve with curd, a small portion of paneer or tofu, or a bowl of dal or sprouts.

Storage and reheating

Refrigerate up to 2 days.
