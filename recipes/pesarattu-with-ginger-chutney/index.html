Pesarattu with Ginger Chutney

BreakfastVegetarianHigh proteinB04

Andhra Pradesh's green crepe uses whole green moong with its skin, so it is unusually high in fibre as well as protein.

Ingredients

  • Batter: 150 g whole green moong, dry (¾ cup) · 15 g raw rice (optional, for crispness) · 10 g ginger · 2 green chillies · ½ tsp cumin · 3 g salt
  • Topping: 60 g onion, finely chopped · 2 tbsp coriander
  • Cooking: 10 g groundnut oil (2 tsp)
  • Ginger chutney: 20 g ginger · 5 g tamarind · 5 g jaggery · 5 g chana dal and 5 g urad dal · 2 dry red chillies · 3 g oil · ½ tsp mustard seeds · salt

Method

  1. Soak moong and rice in 600 ml water for 6 hours or overnight. Drain.
  2. Grind with ginger, chillies, cumin and 100 ml water to a smooth batter, slightly thicker than dosa batter. Add salt.
  3. For the chutney, fry chana dal, urad dal and chillies in 2 g oil until golden. Grind with ginger, tamarind, jaggery, salt and 30 ml water. Temper with mustard seeds in 1 g oil.
  4. Heat a tawa on medium-high. Spread 1 ladle (about 100 g) thinly into a 20 cm circle.
  5. Sprinkle a quarter of the onion and coriander, pressing lightly. Drizzle ½ tsp oil around the edge.
  6. Cook 2–3 minutes until the base is crisp and golden. Pesarattu is usually not flipped; fold and serve.
  7. Serve 2 per person with 2 tbsp chutney.

Protein optimisation

  • Whole moong supplies about 17 g per serving.
  • Boost: the Andhra "MLA pesarattu" style folds in a spoon of upma; for protein instead, crumble 30 g paneer per serving over the onion (+5.5 g protein, +80 kcal, +6 g fat).

Variations, serving and storage

  • Regional: Telangana-style — add 1 tsp cumin and a handful of chopped dill (soa) leaves to the batter.
  • Substitution: split green moong (chilka) soaks in 2 hours and gives a smoother batter.
  • Storage: batter keeps 2 days refrigerated; chutney keeps 5 days refrigerated.
  • Food safety: discard batter with any off smell. Soaked moong left at room temperature longer than 12 hours in hot weather may start to ferment; refrigerate it.
  • Meal prep: excellent — grind batter for two days at once.
