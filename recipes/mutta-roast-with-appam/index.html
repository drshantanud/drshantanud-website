Mutta Roast with Appam

LunchContains eggGood sourceL07

Kerala's egg roast: boiled eggs in a deeply caramelised onion masala, scented with curry leaves and coconut oil, eaten with lacy appam.

Ingredients

  • 6 large eggs, hard-boiled and peeled
  • 200 g onion, thinly sliced · 80 g tomato · 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste · 2 green chillies · 15 curry leaves
  • 15 g coconut oil (1 tbsp) · ½ tsp fennel seeds · 2 tsp Kashmiri chilli powder · 1 tsp coriander powder · ¼ tsp turmeric · ½ tsp crushed pepper · ½ tsp garam masala · 4 g salt
  • Appam (4): batter from 100 g raw rice, 20 g grated coconut, 5 g sugar, ¼ tsp yeast, salt — or 4 ready appams

Method

  1. For appam, soak rice 4 hours; grind with coconut and 150 ml water to a smooth batter. Stir in sugar, yeast and salt; ferment 8 hours.
  2. Boil eggs 10 minutes; cool in cold water and peel. Make 3 shallow slits on each.
  3. Heat coconut oil. Add fennel and curry leaves, then onion. Cook on medium 15 minutes, stirring often, until deep brown and jammy. This step defines the dish; don't rush it.
  4. Add ginger-garlic paste and chillies for 2 minutes. Add tomato and spices; cook 5 minutes until the tomato melts.
  5. Add the eggs and 50 ml water. Toss to coat; cook 3 minutes until the masala clings.
  6. Heat an appam pan (appachatti) on medium. Pour a ladle of batter and swirl to coat the sides. Cover and cook 2 minutes until the centre is spongy and the edges crisp.
  7. Serve the roast with 2 appams each.

Protein optimisation

  • Eggs supply 19 g per serving; appam 4 g; onion and tomato 1.5 g.
  • Boost: add 100 g kala chana (cooked) to the masala with the eggs (+4 g protein, +70 kcal per serving), a common home variation.
  • Lighter version: groundnut oil instead of coconut oil saves about 5 g saturated fat per serving.

Variations, serving and storage

  • Regional: Malabar style — add ½ tsp garam masala and a handful of mint at the end.
  • Substitution: 2 whole-wheat chapatis or idiyappam instead of appam.
  • Storage: roast keeps 1 day refrigerated; appam is best fresh.
  • Food safety: cool boiled eggs quickly and refrigerate within 2 hours if not using.
  • Meal prep: make the masala 2 days ahead; add eggs on the day.
