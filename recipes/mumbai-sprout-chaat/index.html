Mumbai Sprout Chaat

SnackVegetarianHigh proteinS02

Mumbai street chaat made at home: steamed moong and matki sprouts, crunchy vegetables, whisked curd and both chutneys, topped with roasted chana instead of sev.

Ingredients

  • 80 g sprouted moong (from 40 g dry) · 80 g sprouted matki (from 40 g dry)
  • 40 g onion · 60 g tomato · 50 g cucumber, all finely chopped · 50 g boiled potato, diced · 20 g pomegranate seeds
  • 100 g curd, whisked with a pinch of salt and roasted cumin
  • 15 g tamarind-date chutney · 15 g green chutney · 1 tsp chaat masala · ½ tsp black salt · 1 tbsp lemon juice · coriander
  • 20 g roasted chana (dalia), lightly crushed

Method

  1. Steam both sprouts together for 5 minutes until just tender but still crunchy. Cool.
  2. Toss sprouts with onion, tomato, cucumber, potato, chaat masala, black salt and lemon.
  3. Divide between two bowls. Spoon over the curd, then both chutneys.
  4. Top with roasted chana, pomegranate and coriander. Serve immediately.

Protein optimisation

  • Sprouts supply 9 g per serving, curd 2 g, roasted chana 2 g.
  • Boost: add 40 g crumbled paneer per bowl (+7 g protein, +105 kcal, +8 g fat).

Variations, serving and storage

  • Regional: Delhi-style — use boiled kala chana and a pinch of kala namak with ginger juliennes.
  • Substitution: all moong sprouts if matki is unavailable.
  • Storage: assemble just before eating; sprouts keep 3 days refrigerated.
  • Food safety: steam sprouts rather than serving them raw, especially for older adults, pregnant women and young children.
  • Meal prep: prepare sprouts and chopped vegetables ahead; add curd and chutneys at serving.
