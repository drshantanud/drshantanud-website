Gujarati Kadhi

GujaratiTangy, Sweetish, SavouryMild spice

A thin, tangy curd and besan soup with a light spicing.

Regional background: A traditional Gujarati dish, usually slightly sweet.

Serves 4 · Total time 25 min · Easy · Method: Sautéed
Equipment: kadhai or non-stick pan, knife and chopping board

Method

  1. Whisk curd, besan and 3 cups water until smooth.
  2. Add salt, ginger and chilli.
  3. Simmer on low heat 10 to 12 minutes, stirring.
  4. Temper ghee with mustard, cloves and curry leaves and pour over.

Substitutions

  • No jaggery here; add a little if you prefer a sweeter kadhi.

Tips

  • Serve with moong khichdi for a balanced meal.
  • To raise protein, serve with curd, a small portion of paneer or tofu, or a bowl of dal or sprouts.

Storage and reheating

Refrigerate up to 2 days.
