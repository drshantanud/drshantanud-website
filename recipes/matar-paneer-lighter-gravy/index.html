Matar Paneer, Lighter Gravy

LunchVegetarianGood sourceL15

The North Indian favourite, with a tomato-onion gravy thickened by a few cashews instead of cream; the paneer and peas stay the stars.

Ingredients

  • 200 g full-fat paneer, in 2 cm cubes · 150 g green peas (fresh or frozen)
  • Gravy: 150 g onion · 250 g tomato · 10 g cashews, soaked 15 minutes · 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste · 1 green chilli
  • 12 g groundnut oil · 1 tsp cumin seeds · 1 bay leaf · ½ tsp turmeric · 1 tsp Kashmiri chilli powder · 1½ tsp coriander powder · ½ tsp garam masala · 1 tsp kasuri methi · 5 g salt · 200 ml water
  • Plate: 210 g whole-wheat atta for 6 rotis

Method

  1. Blend onion, tomato, cashews, ginger-garlic paste and chilli into a smooth purée.
  2. Heat oil; add cumin and bay leaf. Add the purée and cook on medium 12–15 minutes, stirring, until it darkens, thickens and oil separates at the edges.
  3. Add turmeric, chilli and coriander powders; cook 2 minutes.
  4. Add peas, salt and 200 ml water. Simmer 5 minutes.
  5. Add paneer and simmer 3 minutes only, so it stays soft.
  6. Finish with garam masala and crushed kasuri methi. Serve with rotis.

Protein optimisation

  • Paneer supplies 12 g per serving, peas 3.5 g, gravy 2 g.
  • Boost: serve with 100 g curd (+3.5 g protein, +60 kcal).
  • Low-fat paneer (check the label) saves about 70 kcal and 8 g fat per serving, with similar protein.

Variations, serving and storage

  • Regional: Punjabi dhaba style — add 1 tsp butter at the end (+25 kcal per serving).
  • Substitution: firm tofu for paneer (lower fat, similar protein; adds soy).
  • Storage: 2 days refrigerated.
  • Food safety: refrigerate within 2 hours.
  • Meal prep: make the gravy base ahead; add paneer on the day.
