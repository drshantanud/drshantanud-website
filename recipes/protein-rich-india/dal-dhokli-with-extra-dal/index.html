Dal Dhokli with Extra Dal

LunchVegetarianGood sourceL05

Gujarat's one-pot comfort meal: spiced wheat-and-besan dumplings simmered in sweet-sour toor dal. Here the dal outweighs the dhokli, rather than the reverse.

Ingredients

  • Dal: 120 g toor dal · 100 g tomato · 15 g raw peanuts · 10 g jaggery · 5 g tamarind (or 4 kokum) · ½ tsp turmeric · 1 tsp red chilli powder · 1 tsp dhana-jeera powder · 5 g salt · about 900 ml water in total
  • Dhokli: 90 g whole-wheat atta · 20 g besan · 3 g oil · ¼ tsp ajwain · ¼ tsp turmeric · ½ tsp red chilli powder · pinch of salt · about 50 ml water
  • Tempering: 10 g groundnut oil · ½ tsp mustard seeds · ½ tsp cumin · 2 cloves · 1 small cinnamon stick · 2 dry red chillies · pinch of hing · 8 curry leaves

Method

  1. Pressure-cook toor dal and peanuts with turmeric and 450 ml water for 4 whistles. Whisk the dal smooth; the peanuts stay whole.
  2. Knead the dhokli ingredients into a firm dough. Rest 10 minutes. Roll into a thin 25 cm circle and cut into 4 cm diamonds.
  3. Heat the oil; add all tempering ingredients for 30 seconds. Add tomato and cook 3 minutes.
  4. Add the dal, 450 ml water, jaggery, tamarind, spices and salt. Bring to a rolling boil.
  5. Drop the dhokli pieces in one at a time, stirring gently so they don't stick together.
  6. Simmer 12–15 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the dhokli is cooked through (no raw floury centre when cut) and the dal coats a spoon.
  7. Rest 5 minutes; it thickens. Serve hot with a spoon of lemon juice and coriander.

Protein optimisation

  • Toor dal supplies 9 g per serving, dhokli dough 4.5 g, peanuts 1.5 g.
  • Boost: increase toor to 160 g (+3 g protein, +45 kcal per serving), and serve with chaas made from 150 g curd.

Variations, serving and storage

  • Regional: Kathiawadi version — less jaggery, more chilli and garlic.
  • Substitution: millet dhokli with 50 g jowar flour and 40 g atta.
  • Storage: 1 day refrigerated; the dhokli soaks up liquid, so reheat with water.
  • Food safety: reheat until boiling.
  • Meal prep: moderate; best fresh.
