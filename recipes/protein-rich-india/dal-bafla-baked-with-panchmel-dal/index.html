Dal Bafla (Baked) with Panchmel Dal

LunchVegetarianGood sourceL06

Madhya Pradesh's answer to Rajasthan's dal baati: wheat dumplings boiled first, then baked, served with a five-dal mix. This version brushes the bafla with ghee rather than soaking it.

Ingredients

  • Panchmel dal: 30 g each of toor, chana, moong, urad and masoor dal (150 g total) · 60 g onion · 100 g tomato · 10 g ghee · 1 tsp cumin · pinch of hing · 2 dry red chillies · ½ tsp turmeric · 1 tsp red chilli powder · 1 tsp coriander powder · ½ tsp garam masala · 5 g salt
  • Bafla: 135 g whole-wheat atta · 15 g fine rava · 30 g curd · 10 g ghee (in the dough) · ½ tsp ajwain · ¼ tsp turmeric · 3 g salt · about 70 ml water · 5 g ghee to brush
  • Plate: 300 g curd (100 g each)

Method

  1. Soak the dals together for 1 hour. Pressure-cook with turmeric and 600 ml water for 4 whistles.
  2. Knead the bafla ingredients into a firm dough. Rest 15 minutes. Divide into 6 balls; press a thumbprint into each.
  3. Boil a large pan of water. Drop in the bafla and boil 15 minutes, until they float and look slightly translucent. Drain.
  4. Bake at 200 °C for 20–25 minutes, turning once, until golden with small cracks. (Or cook in an appe pan, covered, on medium-low, 15–20 minutes.)
  5. Meanwhile, heat ghee for the dal; add cumin, hing and red chillies, then onion for 4 minutes and tomato with spices for 5 minutes. Add the dal and 200 ml water; simmer 10 minutes.
  6. Crack each bafla open, brush with ghee, and pour dal over. Serve with curd.

Protein optimisation

  • Panchmel dal supplies 12 g per serving; bafla 6 g. Wheat and five dals complement each other's amino acids.
  • Boost: add 25 g besan to the bafla dough (+2 g protein per serving), or serve with 150 g hung-curd raita instead of curd.

Variations, serving and storage

  • Regional: Rajasthani dal baati — bake without boiling; serve with churma (not counted here).
  • Substitution: any equal mix of dals works; keep chana and urad for body.
  • Storage: dal keeps 3 days refrigerated; bafla 2 days refrigerated, re-crisped in the oven.
  • Food safety: reheat dal until boiling.
  • Meal prep: good — boil the bafla ahead and bake before serving.
