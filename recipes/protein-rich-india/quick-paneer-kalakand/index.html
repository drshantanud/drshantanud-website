Quick Paneer Kalakand

DessertVegetarianGood sourceDS11

Alwar's grainy, milky kalakand made the quick way — crumbled paneer cooked with milk and milk powder until it sets — with far less sugar than the halwai version.

Region
Rajasthan (Alwar)

Ingredients

  • 200 g full-fat paneer, finely crumbled
  • 200 ml toned milk · 40 g skimmed milk powder · 40 g sugar
  • ½ tsp cardamom powder · a pinch of saffron (optional) · 10 g pistachios, slivered

Method

  1. In a heavy non-stick pan, combine paneer, milk and milk powder. Stir until no milk-powder lumps remain.
  2. Cook on medium-low, stirring and scraping constantly, for 10–12 minutes, until most of the liquid evaporates and the mixture thickens.
  3. Add sugar; it will loosen the mixture. Keep cooking 3–5 minutes until it gathers into a soft mass that leaves the sides of the pan.
  4. Stir in cardamom and saffron. Spread in a greased 12 cm square tin, 2–2.5 cm thick. Press pistachios on top.
  5. Cool, then chill 2 hours. Cut into 6 pieces.

Protein optimisation

  • Paneer supplies 6 g per piece, milk powder 2.5 g, milk 1 g.
  • Low-fat paneer (check the label) saves about 25 kcal and 3 g fat per piece with similar protein.

Variations, serving and storage

  • Regional: mango kalakand — fold in 60 g Alphonso pulp at step 3 and reduce sugar to 30 g.
  • Substitution: fresh chhena (DS3) in place of paneer for a softer, more traditional grain.
  • Storage: 4 days refrigerated.
  • Food safety: refrigerate once cool.
  • Meal prep: excellent.
