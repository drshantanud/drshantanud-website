Kanda Poha with Sprouted Moong and Peanuts

BreakfastVegetarianGood sourceB11

Maharashtra's most familiar breakfast, given more protein with a generous handful of sprouted moong folded in at the end.

Ingredients

  • 100 g thick poha (1⅓ cups)
  • 100 g sprouted moong (from about 45 g dry whole moong)
  • 25 g raw peanuts · 80 g onion, finely chopped
  • 10 g groundnut oil (2 tsp) · ½ tsp mustard seeds · 10 curry leaves · 2 green chillies · ¼ tsp turmeric · 3 g salt · ½ tsp sugar (optional, traditional)
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice · 2 tbsp coriander · 10 g fresh coconut (optional garnish)

Method

  1. To sprout: soak moong 8 hours, drain, and keep in a covered colander for 24–36 hours, rinsing twice a day, until tails are 3–5 mm.
  2. Rinse poha in a colander under running water for 20 seconds. Drain and leave 10 minutes to soften. It should be soft but not mushy.
  3. Steam the sprouts for 5 minutes, or microwave with 2 tbsp water for 2 minutes, until just tender.
  4. Heat oil on medium. Fry peanuts 2 minutes until golden; remove.
  5. In the same oil, add mustard seeds; when they pop, add curry leaves and chillies, then onion. Cook 3 minutes until translucent.
  6. Add turmeric, salt and sugar (if using), then poha and sprouts. Toss gently on low heat for 2–3 minutes until heated through.
  7. Turn off the heat. Add peanuts, lemon juice and coriander. Serve with coconut on top.

Protein optimisation

  • Sprouted moong gives about 5 g per serving, peanuts 3 g, poha 3.5 g.
  • Boost: double the sprouts to 200 g (+5 g protein, +75 kcal per serving), or serve with a glass of milk or chaas as above.

Variations, serving and storage

  • Regional: Indori poha — finish with ½ tsp jeeravan masala and a few pomegranate seeds.
  • Substitution: sprouted matki (moth bean) in place of moong for a nuttier taste.
  • Storage: best fresh; keeps 1 day refrigerated, reheated with a sprinkle of water.
  • Food safety: sprouts can carry bacteria; steam or microwave them as described rather than eating raw, especially for older adults, pregnant women and young children. Discard sprouts that smell sour or look slimy.
  • Meal prep: sprout a batch for the week; refrigerated sprouts keep 3 days.
