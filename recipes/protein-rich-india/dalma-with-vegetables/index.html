Dalma with Vegetables

LunchVegetarianGood sourceL11

Odisha's everyday dal: toor dal cooked together with chunks of raw papaya, pumpkin, brinjal and plantain, finished with coconut and roasted cumin-chilli powder.

Ingredients

  • 150 g toor dal (arhar)
  • 300 g mixed vegetables in 3 cm chunks: raw papaya, pumpkin, brinjal, raw plantain, 50 g potato, a few beans
  • 15 g fresh grated coconut · 10 g ghee · 1 tsp panch phutana · 2 dry red chillies · 1 bay leaf · 10 g ginger, grated · ½ tsp turmeric · 5 g salt
  • Bhaja masala: 1 tsp cumin and 2 dry red chillies, dry-roasted and ground
  • Plate: 180 g raw rice (60 g each) · 300 g curd

Method

  1. Rinse toor dal. Cook with turmeric, ginger and 600 ml water for 2 whistles.
  2. Open the cooker, add the vegetables and salt, and cook 1 more whistle (or simmer 10–12 minutes) until the vegetables are tender but whole.
  3. Heat ghee; add panch phutana, red chillies and bay leaf. When the seeds crackle, pour over the dalma.
  4. Stir in coconut and bhaja masala. Simmer 2 minutes.
  5. Serve with rice and curd.

Protein optimisation

  • Toor dal supplies 11 g per serving; vegetables 1 g; rice 4 g and curd 3.5 g on the plate.
  • Boost: add 60 g whole green moong to the dal (+4.5 g protein, +65 kcal per serving), a common Odia home variation.

Variations, serving and storage

  • Regional: Jagannath temple style — no onion, garlic or tomato (already the case here), cooked in ghee.
  • Substitution: any firm seasonal vegetables; avoid leafy greens, which overcook.
  • Storage: 2 days refrigerated.
  • Food safety: reheat until boiling.
  • Meal prep: good.
