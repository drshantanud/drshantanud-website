Vaghareli Khichdi with Kadhi

DinnerVegetarianGood sourceD20

Gujarat's Sunday-night supper: a tempered moong-rice khichdi with vegetables, poured over with thin, sweet-sour Gujarati kadhi.

Ingredients

  • Khichdi: 100 g yellow moong dal (or split green moong) · 60 g rice · 150 g vegetables (50 g peas, potato, carrot, onion) · 8 g ghee · 1 tsp cumin · 2 cloves · 1 bay leaf · pinch of hing · ½ tsp turmeric · 5 g salt · 600 ml water
  • Kadhi: 350 g sour-ish curd · 30 g besan · 400 ml water · 1 tsp ginger-chilli paste · 8 g jaggery · 3 g salt
  • Kadhi tempering: 5 g ghee · ½ tsp mustard seeds · ½ tsp cumin · 2 cloves · 1 small cinnamon stick · 1 dry red chilli · 8 curry leaves · pinch of hing

Method

  1. Rinse moong and rice; soak 15 minutes.
  2. Heat 8 g ghee in a pressure cooker; add cumin, cloves, bay leaf and hing. Add vegetables and turmeric; cook 2 minutes.
  3. Add the dal-rice, salt and water. Pressure-cook 3 whistles. Rest until the pressure drops; stir to a soft, spoonable consistency.
  4. For kadhi, whisk curd, besan and water until completely smooth. Add ginger-chilli paste, jaggery and salt.
  5. Heat on medium, stirring constantly, until it comes to a boil (this stops it splitting). Simmer 8–10 minutes until slightly thickened and no longer tastes of raw besan.
  6. Heat 5 g ghee; add the tempering ingredients and pour over the kadhi.
  7. Serve khichdi with kadhi poured over.

Protein optimisation

  • Moong dal supplies 8 g per serving, curd 4 g, besan 2 g, rice 1.5 g, vegetables 1.5 g.
  • Boost: stir 50 g crumbled paneer per serving into the khichdi (+9 g protein, +130 kcal), or serve with a side of 75 g hung-curd raita (+6 g protein, +75 kcal) — either lifts the plate above 20 g.

Variations, serving and storage

  • Regional: Kathiawadi version — spicier kadhi with garlic, less jaggery.
  • Substitution: little millet (kutki) or broken wheat for rice.
  • Storage: khichdi and kadhi keep 2 days refrigerated, separately.
  • Food safety: cool khichdi within 1 hour; reheat kadhi gently, stirring, until it simmers.
  • Meal prep: good.
