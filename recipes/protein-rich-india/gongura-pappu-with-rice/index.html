Gongura Pappu with Rice

LunchVegetarianGood sourceL09

The signature dal of Andhra Pradesh and Telangana: toor dal cooked with sour gongura (sorrel) leaves and a garlicky red-chilli tempering.

Ingredients

  • 150 g toor dal · 100 g gongura leaves, picked (about 2 packed cups)
  • 60 g onion, sliced · 3 green chillies · ¼ tsp turmeric · 5 g salt
  • Tempering: 12 g groundnut oil · ½ tsp mustard seeds · ½ tsp cumin · 3 dry red chillies · 6 garlic cloves, crushed · 8 curry leaves · pinch of hing
  • Plate: 150 g raw rice (50 g each) · 300 g curd (100 g each)

Method

  1. Pressure-cook toor dal with turmeric and 450 ml water for 4 whistles. Mash.
  2. Cook gongura, onion and green chillies in a pan with 2 tbsp water for 6–8 minutes, until the leaves collapse into a dark green mass.
  3. Add the dal, salt and 200 ml water. Simmer 5 minutes, whisking so the gongura blends through.
  4. Heat oil; add mustard, cumin, red chillies, garlic, curry leaves and hing. Fry until the garlic turns golden.
  5. Pour the tempering over the dal. Serve with rice and curd.

Protein optimisation

  • Toor dal supplies 11 g per serving, rice 3.5 g, curd 3.5 g.
  • Boost: increase toor to 180 g (+2 g protein, +35 kcal per serving), and/or serve 150 g curd rather than 100 g.

Variations, serving and storage

  • Regional: Telangana style — add 1 tsp tamarind and a little more garlic; or use spinach for palakura pappu.
  • Substitution: if gongura is unavailable, use 100 g spinach plus 2 tsp tamarind pulp for sourness.
  • Storage: 2 days refrigerated.
  • Food safety: reheat until boiling.
  • Meal prep: good.
