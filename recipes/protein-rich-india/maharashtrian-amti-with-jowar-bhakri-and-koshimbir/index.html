Maharashtrian Amti with Jowar Bhakri and Koshimbir

LunchVegetarianGood sourceL04

A Maharashtrian everyday plate: sweet-sour toor dal flavoured with goda masala and kokum, a hand-patted jowar bhakri, and a peanut-curd koshimbir salad.

Ingredients

  • Amti: 150 g toor dal · 4 kokum petals (or 1 tsp tamarind) · 8 g jaggery · 5 g goda masala · 10 g fresh coconut · 10 g groundnut oil · ½ tsp mustard seeds · ½ tsp cumin · pinch of hing · 10 curry leaves · ¼ tsp turmeric · 5 g salt · coriander
  • Bhakri: 180 g jowar flour · about 130 ml hot water · pinch of salt
  • Koshimbir: 180 g cucumber · 120 g tomato · 60 g onion · 225 g curd · 30 g roasted peanut powder · green chilli · salt

Method

  1. Pressure-cook toor dal with turmeric and 450 ml water for 4 whistles. Whisk smooth.
  2. Heat oil; add mustard, cumin, hing, curry leaves. Add dal, 300 ml water, kokum, jaggery, goda masala, coconut and salt. Simmer 10 minutes until slightly thickened.
  3. For bhakri, knead jowar flour and salt with hot water, one portion at a time, until smooth and crack-free.
  4. Pat each ball with wet palms (or between plastic sheets) into a 15 cm round.
  5. Cook on a hot tawa 1 minute, brush the top with water, flip and cook 1 minute. Then cook directly over the flame, or press on the tawa, until it puffs and browns.
  6. Mix the koshimbir just before serving to keep the cucumber crisp.

Protein optimisation

  • Toor dal supplies 11 g per serving, bhakri 6 g, koshimbir 6 g (curd and peanuts).
  • Boost: increase toor to 180 g (+2 g protein, +35 kcal per serving), or add 50 g crumbled paneer to the koshimbir (+9 g protein, +130 kcal).

Variations, serving and storage

  • Regional: Kolhapuri amti — add 1 tsp kanda-lasun masala and omit jaggery.
  • Substitution: bajra or rice bhakri; or 2 whole-wheat chapatis.
  • Storage: amti keeps 2 days refrigerated; bhakri is best fresh; koshimbir must be eaten the day it is made.
  • Food safety: keep koshimbir chilled; do not hold it at room temperature for more than 2 hours.
  • Meal prep: make amti ahead; cook bhakri fresh.
