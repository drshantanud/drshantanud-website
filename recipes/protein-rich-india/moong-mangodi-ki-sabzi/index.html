Moong Mangodi ki Sabzi

DinnerVegetarianGood sourceD17

A Rajasthani pantry curry: sun-dried moong dal dumplings (mangodi) roasted and simmered with tomato, peas and a little curd — the desert kitchen's way of eating dal as a vegetable.

Ingredients

  • 80 g moong dal mangodi (sun-dried)
  • 150 g tomato, puréed · 60 g onion, finely chopped (optional; Marwari homes often omit) · 80 g green peas · 60 g curd · 1 tsp grated ginger · 1 green chilli
  • 12 g groundnut or mustard oil · ½ tsp cumin seeds · pinch of hing · ½ tsp turmeric · 1 tsp red chilli powder · 1½ tsp coriander powder · ½ tsp garam masala · 4 g salt · coriander
  • Plate: 210 g whole-wheat or bajra atta for 6 rotis · 300 g curd

Method

  1. Heat 5 g oil; roast mangodi on low for 3–4 minutes, stirring, until golden and fragrant. Remove.
  2. Heat the remaining oil; add cumin and hing, then onion (if using) for 3 minutes, then ginger and chilli.
  3. Add tomato purée and powdered spices. Cook 5 minutes until oil separates.
  4. Lower the heat; stir in whisked curd and cook 1 minute.
  5. Add mangodi, peas, salt and 350 ml water. Cover and simmer 12–15 minutes until the mangodi are soft right through (press one with a spoon).
  6. Finish with garam masala and coriander. Serve with rotis and curd.

Protein optimisation

  • Mangodi supply 6.5 g per serving, peas 2 g, curd 1 g; rotis 8 g and curd 3.5 g on the plate.
  • Boost: increase mangodi to 120 g (+3 g protein, +45 kcal per serving).

Variations, serving and storage

  • Regional: mangodi-aloo — add 1 diced potato for a heartier curry.
  • Substitution: if mangodi are unavailable, use 80 g moong dal soaked 1 hour and cooked with the masala (softer texture, similar protein).
  • Storage: 2 days refrigerated.
  • Food safety: refrigerate within 2 hours.
  • Meal prep: good.
