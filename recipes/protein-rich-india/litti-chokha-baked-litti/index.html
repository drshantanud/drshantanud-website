Litti Chokha (Baked Litti)

LunchVegetarianGood sourceL12

Bihar's best-known meal: whole-wheat balls stuffed with spiced sattu, baked until crusty and served with chokha, a smoky mash of roasted brinjal, tomato and potato.

Region
Bihar

Ingredients

  • Dough: 200 g whole-wheat atta · 10 g ghee · ¼ tsp ajwain · 3 g salt · about 120 ml water
  • Filling: 120 g chana sattu · 50 g onion, finely chopped · 1 tbsp grated garlic and ginger · 2 green chillies · 2 tbsp coriander · ½ tsp ajwain · ½ tsp kalonji · 10 g achar (pickle) masala · 1 tbsp lemon juice · 10 g mustard oil · 3 g salt · 2–3 tbsp water
  • Chokha: 300 g brinjal (1 large) · 200 g tomato · 150 g potato, boiled · 10 g mustard oil · 30 g onion · 2 garlic cloves · 1 green chilli · coriander · salt
  • To finish: 10 g ghee, melted

Method

  1. Knead the dough ingredients until smooth. Rest 15 minutes.
  2. Mix the filling ingredients; it should hold together when pressed.
  3. Divide dough into 9 balls. Flatten each into a 7 cm cup, fill with sattu mixture, seal and roll smooth.
  4. Bake at 200 °C for 30–35 minutes, turning halfway, until deep golden and hollow-sounding when tapped. (Or cook in a covered appe pan on low for 25 minutes, turning often.)
  5. For chokha, roast the brinjal and tomato directly over a gas flame, turning, until charred all over and soft (10–12 minutes). Cool, peel and mash with potato, mustard oil, onion, garlic, chilli, coriander and salt.
  6. Crack each litti slightly, brush with ghee and serve with chokha.

Protein optimisation

  • Sattu supplies 9 g per serving, wheat 7.5 g, vegetables 3 g.
  • Boost: serve with 100 g curd (+3.5 g protein, +60 kcal), or increase sattu to 150 g (+2 g protein per serving).

Variations, serving and storage

  • Regional: Jharkhand style — serve with a ghugni of dried peas alongside the chokha.
  • Substitution: roasted besan (see B10) if sattu is unavailable.
  • Storage: littis keep 2 days refrigerated, re-crisped in the oven; chokha keeps 1 day.
  • Food safety: chokha contains raw onion and garlic in oil; refrigerate promptly.
  • Meal prep: shape littis ahead and refrigerate up to 12 hours before baking.
