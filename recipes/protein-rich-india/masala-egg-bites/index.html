Masala Egg Bites

SnackContains eggHigh proteinS09

Spiced egg mini-muffins with paneer, spinach and peppers: baked in a muffin tray, steamed in katoris or cooked in an appe pan. Three bites make a portable 16 g protein snack.

Ingredients

  • 6 large eggs (300 g shelled) · 4 egg whites (132 g)
  • 60 g full-fat paneer, crumbled
  • 50 g onion · 60 g capsicum · 50 g tomato (deseeded) · 40 g spinach, all finely chopped · 1 green chilli · 2 tbsp coriander
  • ¼ tsp turmeric · ½ tsp garam masala · ¼ tsp black pepper · 3 g salt
  • 4 g groundnut oil for greasing

Method

  1. Preheat the oven to 180 °C. Grease a 12-cup muffin tray (silicone works best).
  2. Whisk eggs, whites, spices and salt until well blended.
  3. Divide the vegetables and paneer among the cups, then pour in the egg mixture to three-quarters full.
  4. Bake 18–20 minutes until puffed and set; a knife in the centre should come out clean.
  5. No oven: steam in greased steel katoris for 12–14 minutes, or cook in a covered, greased appe pan on low for 8–10 minutes, turning once.
  6. Cool 5 minutes before unmoulding; they deflate slightly.

Protein optimisation

  • Eggs and whites supply 13 g per serving; paneer 2.5 g.
  • Lighter swap: replace 2 whole eggs with 4 egg whites (similar protein; about 20 kcal and 2.5 g fat less per serving). Boost: add 40 g more paneer (+2 g protein, +25 kcal per serving).

Variations, serving and storage

  • Regional: Parsi-inspired — add a little chopped green garlic and ¼ tsp cumin, as in akuri.
  • Substitution: grated cheese in place of paneer (check salt).
  • Storage: 3 days refrigerated; reheat 30 seconds in a microwave or 3 minutes in a steamer.
  • Food safety: refrigerate within 2 hours of cooking; carry chilled if eaten more than 4 hours later.
  • Meal prep: excellent.
