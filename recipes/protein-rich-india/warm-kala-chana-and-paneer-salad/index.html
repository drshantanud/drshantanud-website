Warm Kala Chana and Paneer Salad

DinnerVegetarianHigh proteinD19

A modern warm salad: kala chana and tawa-seared paneer tossed with crunchy vegetables, roasted pumpkin seeds and a lemon-chaat masala dressing. A complete dinner in a bowl, no staple needed.

High protein. IFCT 2017 has no lettuce or pumpkin-seed entry; spinach and sunflower seeds are used as the nearest equivalents. Values checked against IFCT 2017 on 14 Oct 2026.

Ingredients

  • 80 g kala chana, dry (about 200 g cooked) · 100 g full-fat paneer, in 1.5 cm cubes
  • 100 g cucumber · 80 g tomato · 40 g onion · 60 g capsicum, all diced · 50 g lettuce or baby spinach
  • 10 g roasted pumpkin seeds · 2 tbsp mint and coriander
  • Dressing: 8 g groundnut or cold-pressed mustard oil · 2 tbsp lemon juice · 1 tsp chaat masala · ½ tsp roasted cumin · ¼ tsp black salt · pinch of chilli flakes

Method

  1. Soak kala chana 8 hours; pressure-cook with 400 ml water and a pinch of salt for 5–6 whistles until tender. Drain.
  2. Sear paneer cubes on a dry non-stick tawa for 1 minute per side until golden.
  3. Warm the chana in the same pan for 2 minutes with a pinch of chaat masala.
  4. Whisk the dressing.
  5. Toss greens and vegetables with half the dressing. Top with warm chana and paneer, the remaining dressing, pumpkin seeds and herbs. Serve immediately.

Protein optimisation

  • Paneer supplies 9 g per serving, kala chana 7.5 g, pumpkin seeds 1.5 g, vegetables 1.5 g.
  • Boost: add 50 g sprouted moong (steamed) per bowl (+3.5 g protein, +40 kcal).
  • Lighter: low-fat paneer saves about 35 kcal and 5 g fat per serving.

Variations, serving and storage

  • Regional: Delhi chana chaat — add a spoon of tamarind chutney and boiled potato (adds about 70 kcal).
  • Substitution: kabuli chana, rajma or tofu.
  • Storage: chana and paneer keep 3 days refrigerated; dress just before eating.
  • Food safety: keep paneer chilled until searing; pack dressing separately for lunch boxes.
  • Meal prep: excellent.
