Baked Soya-Vegetable Cutlets

SnackVegetarianHigh proteinS07

Soy granules, potato and vegetables shaped into crisp cutlets with an oat crust and baked or shallow-fried with just 12 g oil for the batch.

Ingredients

  • 80 g soy granules, dry
  • 150 g potato, boiled and mashed · 60 g carrot, finely grated · 50 g green peas, lightly mashed · 50 g onion, finely chopped · 40 g beans, finely chopped
  • 30 g besan, dry-roasted 2 minutes · 1 tsp ginger-garlic paste · 1 green chilli · ½ tsp garam masala · 1 tsp chaat masala · ½ tsp amchur · 2 tbsp coriander · 4 g salt
  • Coating: 30 g rolled oats, pulsed coarsely
  • 12 g groundnut oil for brushing

Method

  1. Soak soy granules in 300 ml hot water for 10 minutes. Drain and squeeze out all the water in a cloth — wet granules make soft, crumbly cutlets.
  2. Sauté onion, carrot, beans and peas in 2 g oil for 4 minutes until dry.
  3. Mix granules, vegetables, potato, besan, spices, coriander and salt into a firm mixture.
  4. Shape into 8 patties, 7 cm across and 1.5 cm thick. Press both sides into the oats.
  5. Bake: place on a greased tray, brush with the remaining oil, and bake at 200 °C for 20–25 minutes, turning once, until golden and crisp. Tawa: shallow-fry on medium, 3 minutes per side, using the same 10 g oil.
  6. Serve with green chutney or hung-curd dip.

Protein optimisation

  • Soy granules supply 10.5 g per serving; besan 1.5 g; oats 1 g; vegetables 2 g.
  • Boost: serve with 75 g hung-curd mint dip (+4 g protein, +70 kcal).

Variations, serving and storage

  • Regional: Kolkata "veg chop" style — add 50 g grated beetroot and a pinch of roasted cumin; coat in breadcrumbs.
  • Substitution: gluten-free version with certified gluten-free oats, or roll in 20 g rice flakes (poha) pulsed coarsely.
  • Storage: cooked cutlets keep 3 days refrigerated; shaped raw cutlets freeze 1 month.
  • Food safety: reheat until hot throughout (75 °C at the centre).
  • Meal prep: excellent.
