Anda Paratha

BreakfastContains eggHigh proteinB18

Delhi's street-side egg paratha: a whole-wheat paratha with beaten egg cooked onto one face, crisp outside and soft within.

Ingredients

  • Dough: 100 g whole-wheat atta · about 65 ml water · pinch of salt
  • 4 large eggs (200 g shelled) · 40 g onion, finely chopped · 1 green chilli · 2 tbsp coriander · ¼ tsp red chilli powder · pinch of black pepper · 2 g salt
  • 10 g groundnut oil (2 tsp)

Method

  1. Knead atta, salt and water into a soft dough. Rest 15 minutes. Divide into 2 balls.
  2. Beat the eggs with onion, chilli, coriander, spices and salt.
  3. Roll each ball to a 20 cm circle. Cook on a hot tawa on medium-high, 30 seconds per side, until it just firms up.
  4. Brush the top with ½ tsp oil. Pour half the egg mixture onto the tawa beside the paratha and spread to its size.
  5. Immediately lay the paratha, oiled side down, on the egg and press gently so they bond.
  6. Cook 2 minutes on medium until the egg sets. Flip so the egg side faces up; brush with ½ tsp oil and cook 1–2 minutes until the base is golden.
  7. Flip once more for 30 seconds to crisp the egg. Cut into quarters and serve with green chutney and onion rings.

Protein optimisation

  • Eggs supply 12.5 g per serving, wheat 5.5 g.
  • Boost: add 1 egg white per paratha to the egg mixture (+3.5 g protein, +17 kcal per serving), or serve with 100 g curd (+3.5 g protein, +60 kcal).

Variations, serving and storage

  • Regional: Old Delhi pocket method — roll a layered paratha, cook briefly until it puffs, slit one edge, pour the egg inside and seal on the tawa. Harder, but fun once mastered.
  • Substitution: half multigrain atta for a nuttier flavour.
  • Storage: best fresh; can be packed for a tiffin within 4 hours.
  • Food safety: cook until the egg layer is fully set and no liquid remains.
  • Meal prep: make the dough the night before; refrigerate.
