Mango Chia Pudding

DessertVegetarianGood sourceDS8

Chia seeds soaked overnight in milk and hung curd, layered with ripe mango: a no-cook dessert with no added sugar and 7 g fibre.

Ingredients

  • 300 ml toned milk · 80 g hung curd · 30 g chia seeds
  • 150 g ripe mango, diced · ¼ tsp cardamom

Method

  1. Whisk milk, hung curd and cardamom until smooth.
  2. Stir in chia seeds. Rest 10 minutes, then stir again to break up any clumps.
  3. Cover and refrigerate at least 4 hours or overnight, until thick and spoonable.
  4. Layer in glasses with the mango. Serve chilled.

Protein optimisation

  • Milk supplies 4.5 g per serving, hung curd 2.5 g, chia 2.5 g.
  • Boost: increase hung curd to 150 g (+2 g protein, +35 kcal per serving) for a thicker pudding.

Variations, serving and storage

  • Seasonal: jamun, strawberries or chopped chikoo in place of mango.
  • Substitution: sabja (basil) seeds soak faster but have less protein and fat.
  • Storage: 2 days refrigerated (add mango on the day).
  • Food safety: always soak chia before eating; dry chia swells and can be a choking hazard.
  • Meal prep: excellent.
