Seasonal Fruit-Yogurt Parfait with Roasted Seeds

DessertVegetarianGood sourceDS9

Layers of thick hung curd, seasonal Indian fruit and crunchy roasted pumpkin and sunflower seeds, with a drizzle of honey and cinnamon.

Good source. The seeds add fat as well as protein, keeping it under 5 g protein per 100 kcal. Crushed roasted chana in place of the seeds (see Substitution) gives about 13 g and makes it High protein, as does the whey boost. Values checked against IFCT 2017 on 15 Oct 2026.

Ingredients

  • 150 g hung curd · 5 g honey · pinch of cinnamon
  • 120 g seasonal fruit: pomegranate seeds, guava, apple, papaya, strawberries or mango, diced
  • 15 g pumpkin and sunflower seeds, dry-roasted 3 minutes

Method

  1. Whisk the hung curd with honey and cinnamon until smooth.
  2. In a glass, layer a third of the curd, half the fruit and a third of the seeds; repeat; finish with curd and seeds.
  3. Serve at once, while the seeds are crunchy.

Protein optimisation

  • Hung curd supplies 8.5 g, seeds 4 g, fruit 1 g.
  • Boost: 10 g whey whisked into the curd (+8 g protein, +40 kcal).

Variations, serving and storage

  • Regional flavour: Kesar-pista — saffron and pistachios with mango in summer; chikoo and nutmeg in winter.
  • Substitution: roasted chana (crushed) for seeds (similar protein, less fat; makes it High protein).
  • Storage: assemble just before eating; store seeds separately.
  • Food safety: wash fruit well.
  • Meal prep: prepare curd and fruit ahead; keep seeds dry.
