Bafauri (Steamed Chana Dal Dumplings)

SnackVegetarianGood sourceS08

Chhattisgarh's oil-free answer to pakoras: coarsely ground chana dal with onion and herbs, steamed into small dumplings, then tossed in a light tempering.

Ingredients

  • 150 g chana dal
  • 60 g onion, finely chopped · 2 green chillies · 10 g ginger · 3 tbsp coriander · ½ tsp cumin · ¼ tsp ajwain · 4 g salt
  • Tempering (optional): 6 g groundnut oil · ½ tsp mustard seeds · 5 g sesame seeds · 8 curry leaves

Method

  1. Soak chana dal 4 hours. Drain well.
  2. Grind coarsely with chillies and ginger, without water, to a thick, grainy paste.
  3. Mix in onion, coriander, cumin, ajwain and salt.
  4. Shape into small balls or flattened ovals, about 20 g each, with wet hands.
  5. Steam on a greased plate or idli stand for 12–15 minutes, until firm and a skewer comes out clean.
  6. For the tempering, heat oil; add mustard, sesame and curry leaves, then toss the bafauri for 1 minute. Serve with tomato or coriander chutney.

Protein optimisation

  • Chana dal supplies 10.5 g per serving.
  • Boost: serve with 75 g hung-curd mint dip (+4 g protein, +70 kcal), or replace 30 g chana dal with 30 g moong dal (similar protein, softer texture).

Variations, serving and storage

  • Shape variation: roll into finger-length logs before steaming, then slice and temper with sesame and dry red chillies.
  • Substitution: mixed chana and moong dal.
  • Storage: 2 days refrigerated; re-steam or toss in a hot pan.
  • Food safety: refrigerate within 2 hours.
  • Meal prep: excellent; freezes well for 1 month.
