Hyderabadi Khagina

DinnerContains eggHigh proteinD13

Hyderabad's spiced egg scramble: eggs cooked with browned onion, green chillies, mint and garam masala until just set and slightly dry — bolder and firmer than Parsi akuri.

Ingredients

  • 5 large eggs (250 g shelled) · 2 egg whites (66 g)
  • 120 g onion, finely sliced · 80 g tomato, chopped · 1 tsp ginger-garlic paste · 3 green chillies, chopped · 2 tbsp mint · 2 tbsp coriander
  • 10 g groundnut oil · ½ tsp cumin seeds · ¼ tsp turmeric · ½ tsp red chilli powder · ½ tsp garam masala · 3 g salt
  • Plate: 70 g whole-wheat atta for 2 rotis

Method

  1. Beat eggs and whites with salt and turmeric.
  2. Heat oil; add cumin, then onion. Cook 6–7 minutes on medium until golden brown.
  3. Add ginger-garlic paste and chillies for 1 minute, then tomato and chilli powder; cook 3 minutes until soft.
  4. Pour in the eggs. Let them set for 20 seconds, then stir and fold on medium heat for 2–3 minutes, until set in soft curds with no liquid egg left. Khagina should be drier than akuri.
  5. Add garam masala, mint and coriander; toss 30 seconds. Serve with roti.

Protein optimisation

  • Eggs and whites supply 19.5 g per serving; vegetables 1.5 g; roti 4 g on the plate.
  • Boost: crumble in 50 g paneer with the eggs (+4.5 g protein, +65 kcal per serving).

Variations, serving and storage

  • Regional: Hyderabadi "egg khagina with fried onion" — top with 1 tbsp crisp fried onion (adds about 40 kcal).
  • Substitution: 2 whole-wheat pav instead of roti.
  • Storage: best fresh.
  • Food safety: cook until no liquid egg remains.
  • Meal prep: slice onions ahead.
