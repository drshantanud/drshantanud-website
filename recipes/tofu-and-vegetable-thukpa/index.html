Tofu and Vegetable Thukpa

DinnerVegetarianHigh proteinD11

The noodle soup of Sikkim and the Himalayan belt: whole-wheat noodles, vegetables and seared tofu in a ginger-garlic broth, warmed with timur (Sichuan pepper) and green chilli.

Ingredients

  • 80 g whole-wheat noodles, dry · 200 g firm tofu, in 2 cm cubes
  • 250 g vegetables: cabbage, carrot, beans, spinach, mushrooms, spring onion · 80 g tomato, chopped
  • 10 g garlic and 15 g ginger, finely chopped · 2 green chillies · ¼ tsp timur or Sichuan pepper, crushed (or black pepper)
  • 10 ml soy sauce · 8 g groundnut oil · 900 ml water or vegetable stock · coriander · lemon wedges

Method

  1. Cook noodles to packet instructions, about 1 minute short. Drain and rinse in cold water.
  2. Sear tofu in 4 g oil in a non-stick pan until golden, 5–6 minutes. Remove.
  3. In a pot, heat the remaining oil; add garlic, ginger and chillies for 30 seconds. Add tomato and cook 3 minutes until soft.
  4. Add water, soy sauce and timur. Boil, then add the firmer vegetables (carrot, beans, cabbage) for 4 minutes and the mushrooms and spinach for 1 minute.
  5. Divide noodles and tofu between two bowls; ladle the hot broth and vegetables over. Finish with spring onion, coriander and lemon.

Protein optimisation

  • Tofu supplies 15 g per serving; whole-wheat noodles 5 g; vegetables 2 g.
  • Boost: add 1 soft-boiled egg per bowl for an egg-inclusive version (+6 g protein, +72 kcal), or increase tofu to 300 g (+7.5 g protein, +70 kcal per serving).

Variations, serving and storage

  • Regional: Ladakhi thenthuk — replace noodles with hand-pulled flat pieces of whole-wheat dough cooked in the broth.
  • Substitution: rice noodles and tamari for a gluten-free version.
  • Storage: keep broth, noodles and tofu separately, 2 days refrigerated.
  • Food safety: reheat broth until boiling before pouring over.
  • Meal prep: good — broth base keeps 2 days.
