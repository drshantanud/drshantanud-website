Matar Paneer

North Indian / PunjabiSavoury, SpicyMedium spice

Green peas and paneer in a spiced tomato gravy.

Regional background: A classic Punjabi dish, here with less oil and no cream.

Serves 3 · Total time 30 min · Easy · Method: Sautéed
Equipment: kadhai or non-stick pan, knife and chopping board

Add to a meal:

Method

  1. Sauté onion, ginger and garlic in oil.
  2. Add tomato and spices and cook until soft; blend if you want a smooth gravy.
  3. Add peas and 1/2 cup water and simmer 8 minutes.
  4. Add paneer cubes and cook 3 more minutes.

Substitutions

  • Tofu or low-fat paneer.

Tips

  • Use frozen peas if fresh are out of season.

Storage and reheating

Refrigerate up to 2 days.
