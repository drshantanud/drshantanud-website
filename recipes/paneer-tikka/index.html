Grilled Paneer Tikka

North Indian / PunjabiSpicy, Tangy, SavouryHot spice

Paneer and vegetables marinated in spiced curd and grilled.

Regional background: A traditional Punjabi tandoori preparation, here grilled or air-fried.

Serves 2 · Total time 30 min · Medium · Method: Grilled, Air-fried
Equipment: grill or air fryer, kadhai or non-stick pan, knife and chopping board

Quantities are in the raw or dry state shown, so water absorbed during cooking is not counted twice. Also used, not counted in nutrition (negligible): salt, lemon juice, ginger-garlic paste.

Method

  1. Mix curd, spices, salt, lemon juice and oil into a marinade.
  2. Coat paneer, capsicum and onion pieces and rest 30 minutes.
  3. Thread onto skewers.
  4. Grill or air-fry at 200 C for 10 to 12 minutes, turning once.

Substitutions

  • Firm tofu or low-fat paneer.

Tips

  • Use low-fat paneer and hung curd to lower the fat.

Storage and reheating

Best fresh; refrigerate up to 1 day.
