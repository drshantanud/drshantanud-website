Achari Tofu Tikka

SnackVegetarianHigh proteinS06

Pressed tofu in a hung-curd marinade flavoured with pickling spices — fennel, nigella, mustard and fenugreek seeds. The bold achari flavour suits tofu, which absorbs it well.

Ingredients

  • 300 g firm tofu, pressed and cut into 3 cm cubes
  • 60 g onion, 80 g capsicum, 60 g tomato (deseeded), in 3 cm pieces
  • Achari masala: ½ tsp each fennel, mustard and nigella seeds and ¼ tsp methi seeds, dry-roasted and coarsely ground
  • Marinade: 75 g hung curd · 10 g besan, dry-roasted · 8 g mustard oil · 1 tsp ginger-garlic paste · 1 tsp Kashmiri chilli powder · ½ tsp turmeric · ½ tsp amchur · 1 tbsp lemon juice · 4 g salt

Method

  1. Press tofu between kitchen towels under a heavy pan for 15 minutes. Cut into cubes.
  2. Mix the marinade with the achari masala. Fold in tofu and vegetables. Rest 30 minutes.
  3. Thread onto skewers.
  4. Oven: 230 °C for 15 minutes, turning once. Air-fryer: 200 °C for 10–12 minutes. Tawa: medium-high, 2–3 minutes per side, until golden and lightly charred.
  5. Serve with onion rings and lemon.

Protein optimisation

  • Tofu supplies 15 g per serving; hung curd 2 g; besan and vegetables 1 g.
  • Boost: increase tofu to 400 g (+5 g protein, +50 kcal per serving).

Variations, serving and storage

  • Regional: tandoori-style — skip the achari spices and use the paneer tikka marinade (S05).
  • Substitution: extra-firm tofu holds shape best; silken tofu will not work.
  • Storage: 2 days refrigerated; reheat on a hot tawa.
  • Food safety: keep opened tofu in fresh water, refrigerated, for no more than 3 days.
  • Meal prep: good — marinate up to 12 hours ahead.
