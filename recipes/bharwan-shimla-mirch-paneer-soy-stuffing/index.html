Bharwan Shimla Mirch (Paneer-Soy Stuffing)

DinnerVegetarianHigh proteinD06

Punjabi stuffed capsicums, filled with a spiced paneer and soy-granule mixture and roasted until the peppers soften and char.

Ingredients

  • 3 medium capsicums (about 450 g), halved lengthwise and deseeded
  • Stuffing: 120 g full-fat paneer, crumbled · 40 g soy granules, dry · 60 g onion, finely chopped · 60 g tomato, finely chopped · 40 g green peas · 1 tsp ginger-garlic paste · 1 green chilli · ½ tsp turmeric · 1 tsp Kashmiri chilli powder · 1 tsp amchur · ½ tsp garam masala · 4 g salt · 2 tbsp coriander
  • 10 g groundnut or mustard oil
  • Plate: 105 g whole-wheat atta for 3 rotis · 300 g salad

Method

  1. Soak soy granules in hot water 10 minutes; drain and squeeze dry.
  2. Heat 6 g oil; cook onion 4 minutes. Add ginger-garlic paste and chilli, then tomato and spices; cook 3 minutes.
  3. Add granules and peas; cook 3 minutes. Off the heat, fold in paneer and coriander.
  4. Fill the capsicum halves generously, pressing the stuffing in.
  5. Oven: brush the capsicums with the remaining oil and roast at 200 °C for 20–25 minutes until soft and lightly charred. Pan: cook covered on low in a wide pan, 12–15 minutes, until the capsicums are tender.
  6. Serve with roti and salad.

Protein optimisation

  • Paneer supplies 7 g per serving, soy granules 7 g, peas and capsicum 2 g.
  • Boost: increase soy granules to 60 g (+3.5 g protein, +25 kcal per serving).

Variations, serving and storage

  • Regional: Rajasthani stuffing — add 1 tsp crushed fennel and a pinch of hing.
  • Substitution: large tomatoes or round gourd (tinda) as cases.
  • Storage: 2 days refrigerated; reheat in the oven.
  • Food safety: refrigerate within 2 hours.
  • Meal prep: make the stuffing a day ahead.
