Tofu Bhurji

BreakfastVeganHigh proteinE03

Crumbled firm tofu cooked like egg bhurji with onion, tomato and capsicum. A kala namak pinch gives the familiar eggy flavour.

Ingredients

  • 200 g firm tofu, pressed and crumbled
  • 60 g onion, chopped · 60 g tomato, chopped · 40 g capsicum, chopped
  • 5 g ginger · 5 g garlic · 1 green chilli · 10 g coriander leaves
  • 8 g oil · ½ tsp cumin seeds · ¼ tsp turmeric · ½ tsp pav bhaji masala · ¼ tsp kala namak · 2 g salt

Method

  1. Press the tofu between kitchen towels under a weight for 10 minutes, then crumble with your fingers.
  2. Heat oil, add cumin seeds, then ginger, garlic and chilli for 30 seconds.
  3. Add onion and cook 3 minutes; add capsicum and tomato and cook until soft.
  4. Add turmeric, pav bhaji masala and salt, then the tofu. Stir-fry on high heat 3–4 minutes until dry and lightly browned.
  5. Sprinkle kala namak and coriander; serve hot.

Protein optimisation

  • Tofu supplies about 13 g protein per serving.
  • Boost: add 50 g green peas (+2 g) or serve with 1 phulka and 100 g curd.

Variations, serving and storage

  • Paneer bhurji: use 150 g paneer instead of tofu (see Paneer Bhurji with Capsicum).
  • Tofu protein varies by brand (about 8–16 g per 100 g); check the label.
  • Storage: 1 day refrigerated.
