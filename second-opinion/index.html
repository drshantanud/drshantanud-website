SECOND OPINION

Online Cardiology Second Opinion

Facing a decision about angioplasty, bypass, a valve, a pacemaker or a long-term heart condition? Share your reports and I will review them and get back to you.

Your details

Upload your records

Choose files or drop them here

Reports, discharge summary, ECG, echo, angiography, CT, blood tests. PDF, photos, Word or ZIP. Up to 10 files, 25 MB each.

    How it works

    1. Fill in your details and upload your reports.
    2. Dr. Deshpande reviews your records.
    3. You receive a response by WhatsApp, call or email, with a suggestion on whether a clinic or video consultation is needed.

    Good to know

    • A second opinion based on records helps you decide; it does not replace an examination.
    • Not for emergencies. For chest pain, breathlessness or fainting, go to the nearest hospital now.
    • Prefer to send files directly? WhatsApp or email drshantanud@yahoo.com.
