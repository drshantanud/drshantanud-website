Get a Second Opinion

Have you been advised angioplasty, bypass surgery, TAVI or another heart procedure and want an expert review first? Share your details and upload your reports. Dr. Shantanu Deshpande will review them and the clinic will get back to you.

  • Useful reports: angiogram report or video link, echo, ECG, CT, stress test, discharge summary and current prescriptions.
  • Files are stored privately and are seen only by the clinic team.
  • For chest pain or any emergency, go to the nearest hospital now. Do not wait for a reply.
