About Dr. Shantanu Deshpande

Interventional Cardiologist

  • MBBS – G.S. Medical College & KEM Hospital
  • MD – T.N. Medical College
  • DM Cardiology – Grant Medical College

An interventional cardiologist practising in Thane and Chembur, Mumbai, with more than 25 years of experience in cardiology and advanced cardiovascular interventions.

Areas of expertise

  • Complex coronary interventions
  • Coronary angioplasty and stenting
  • IVUS and OCT-guided interventions
  • Rotablation
  • Intravascular lithotripsy
  • TAVI
  • Structural heart interventions
  • Pacemaker procedures
  • Valvular interventions

Alongside clinical practice, Dr. Deshpande is involved in academic cardiology, teaching, clinical research and patient education.
